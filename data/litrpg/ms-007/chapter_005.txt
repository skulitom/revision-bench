The air in the Cistern Gate was thick with the scent of moss and damp stone. Ilrid stood at the entrance, his boots crunching on gravel as he surveyed the towering archway before him. The gate loomed like a silent guardian, its ancient stones whispering tales of ages past.

Warden Ott, a burly figure with eyes sharp as flint, stepped forward. "You're here to see what lies beyond, aren't you?" his voice rumbled, echoing slightly in the confined space.

Ilrid nodded, feeling the weight of the sealed vial in his pocket. It was a mystery he intended to unravel. "I need to know what's on the other side," he replied, his voice steady despite the fluttering in his chest.

Ott chuckled, a sound that seemed to bounce off the walls. "Many have tried, few have returned. But you've got something they didn't—a map."

Ilrid's heart skipped a beat as Ott extended a hand, revealing a folded map, its edges worn and ink faded. "This is yours," Ott said, sliding it across the stone slab between them. "Use it wisely."

Ilrid took the map, fingers tracing the intricate lines and symbols. It felt like destiny unfolding in his hands. "Thank you," he said, though he knew gratitude was a small price for such knowledge.

Ott nodded, eyes glinting with an unreadable emotion. "Remember, the path is treacherous. Use your skills wisely."

Ilrid pocketed the map next to the vial, feeling a surge of determination. With Ember Lash and Stone Ward at his disposal, he was ready to face whatever lay ahead. The Cistern Gate might be formidable, but Ilrid was prepared to unlock its secrets.

As he stepped through the archway, the world beyond shimmered with possibilities, each step forward a promise of adventure and discovery.
