Ilrid stepped cautiously into the Sallow Wood, the air thick with the scent of damp earth and decaying leaves. The forest was a maze of gnarled trees and tangled undergrowth, shadows dancing in the dappled sunlight that filtered through the canopy above. He clutched the folded map tightly, trying to make sense of the scribbled paths and landmarks.

Ahead, he spotted Warden Ott standing by a fallen tree, his eyes scanning the horizon with an intensity that suggested urgency. Ilrid approached, his boots crunching on the dry leaves beneath him. "Warden Ott," he greeted, keeping his voice steady despite the unease that gnawed at him.

Ott turned, his face lined with concern. "Ilrid," he nodded. "I'm glad you've arrived. The forest is restless tonight."

Ilrid nodded, feeling a chill run down his spine. "What's wrong?"

"The creatures are restless," Ott explained, gesturing to the shadows around them. "They sense something... unnatural."

Ilrid glanced at the sealed vial in his inventory, wondering if it held the key to whatever was amiss. He needed to be ready for anything.

"Stay alert," Ott advised, stepping aside to reveal a narrow path leading deeper into the woods. "This way."

Ilrid followed, his mind racing. He knew he had to rely on his skills—Ember Lash and Stone Ward—to protect himself and uncover whatever threat lurked in the Sallow Wood. The map in his hand seemed more like a puzzle than a guide, but he was determined to solve it.

As they moved forward, Ilrid felt a strange energy in the air, as if the forest itself were watching them. He tightened his grip on the vial, ready to unleash Ember Lash at a moment's notice. The path ahead was uncertain, but he was prepared to face whatever came next.
