The Drowned Steps were a sight to behold, even in their dilapidated state. The once majestic staircase leading down into the depths of the forgotten city was now half-submerged, with water lapping at its moss-covered steps. Ilrid adjusted his pack and took a deep breath, feeling the weight of adventure pressing against him.

"Ready?" Maro's voice echoed from behind, tinged with a hint of nervous excitement. Ilrid nodded, turning to face his companion—a burly figure with eyes that sparkled like polished obsidian.

"Yeah, let's do this," Ilrid replied, his voice steady despite the fluttering in his stomach. They had been training for weeks, preparing for this very moment. The sealed vial in his inventory was their ticket to uncovering secrets hidden beneath the city's watery shroud.

As they descended the first few steps, the air grew cooler and more humid. Ilrid could feel the dampness seeping into his boots, but he pressed on, determined to explore what lay ahead. The path was treacherous, with slippery stones and occasional patches of algae making each step a careful dance.

Maro paused, looking back at Ilrid with an encouraging grin. "You've got this," he said, clapping Ilrid on the shoulder. "Remember, if things get hairy, you've got Ember Lash."

Ilrid nodded, feeling a surge of confidence. He knew his skill was limited, but it was all he had for now. With a deep breath, they continued their descent, the mysteries of the Drowned Steps waiting to be uncovered.
