The sun dipped below the horizon as Ilrid stood at the edge of Highcut, the town's silhouette etched against a canvas of twilight. Bex, his companion, was already there, her eyes scanning the surroundings with practiced ease.

"Looks like we're in for another quiet night," Bex remarked, turning to face him. Her voice carried a hint of anticipation, a contrast to the stillness around them.

Ilrid nodded, pulling the tin lantern from his inventory and igniting it. The warm glow cut through the encroaching darkness, casting dancing shadows on their faces. "Let's make sure it stays that way," he replied, his tone steady.

As they walked through the cobblestone streets, Ilrid felt a familiar tug at his mind—a call to learn. It was an odd sensation, like a whisper from the world itself, urging him forward. He paused, closing his eyes for a moment, letting the sensation wash over him. When he opened them again, Bex was watching him with a curious tilt of her head.

"Something up?" she asked, her voice soft but insistent.

Ilrid shook his head, a small smile playing on his lips. "Just... focusing," he said, and took a deep breath. The world around him seemed to shift subtly, the air charged with potential.

He reached out with his mind, feeling for the knowledge that beckoned. It was there, just beyond reach, a skill waiting to be grasped. With a final push of will, he seized it—Ash Beckon. A surge of power coursed through him, and he felt himself rise to level 4.

Bex's eyes widened in surprise. "Did you just...?"

Ilrid nodded, feeling the new skill settle into his consciousness like a long-lost friend. "Yeah," he said, the thrill of growth still humming in his veins. "Looks like we're ready for whatever comes next."

Together, they continued their patrol through Highcut, the town safe under the watchful eyes of its guardians.
