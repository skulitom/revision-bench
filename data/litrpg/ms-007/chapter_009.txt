Ilrid stood at the edge of Sallow Wood, the eerie silence pressing against his senses. The trees loomed like sentinels, their gnarled branches whispering secrets in the wind. He gripped the tin lantern tightly, its dim light casting long shadows on the forest floor. 

A figure emerged from the mist, tall and gaunt, with eyes that seemed to pierce through the gloom. "Tallyman," Ilrid muttered under his breath, recalling tales of this enigmatic guardian. The Tallyman's presence was both unsettling and familiar, a reminder of the debts owed in this strange world.

"Ilrid," the Tallyman's voice echoed, each word resonating with an ancient authority. "You seek passage through Sallow Wood, yet you hold no map to guide your way."

Ilrid nodded, feeling the weight of his predicament. The sealed vial in his pocket felt heavier than before, its contents unknown but undoubtedly significant.

"Use your Echo Sight," the Tallyman suggested, a hint of amusement in his tone. "The forest hides more than it reveals."

With a deep breath, Ilrid focused, activating his Echo Sight. The world around him shifted subtly, revealing hidden paths and obscured dangers. The trees seemed to part, offering glimpses of the way forward.

As he moved deeper into the wood, the Tallyman's voice lingered in his mind, a constant reminder of the choices that lay ahead. Ilrid knew he must tread carefully, for every step could alter the balance of his journey.
