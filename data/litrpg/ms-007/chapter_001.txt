The Drowned Steps were a sight to behold, even in their decay. The once majestic staircase leading into the depths of the ancient city was now submerged, its stone steps worn and moss-covered. Ilrid stood at the edge, feeling the weight of the water pressing against his chest. It was a place steeped in mystery and danger, but he had come here for a reason.

"Hey, Ilrid," called Maro from behind him, his voice echoing slightly off the submerged walls. "Ready to dive in?"

Ilrid nodded, taking a deep breath. He could feel the adrenaline coursing through his veins, a mix of excitement and trepidation. "Let's do this."

As they descended into the water, Ilrid couldn't shake the feeling that something was watching them. The light from above filtered down in eerie beams, casting long shadows that seemed to dance around them.

Maro swam ahead, his form cutting through the water with practiced ease. Ilrid followed closely, his mind racing with possibilities. What secrets lay beneath these steps? And what dangers?

Suddenly, a shape emerged from the murk—a creature with eyes like burning coals. It lunged at Maro, but Ilrid was ready. He reached into his inventory, fingers brushing against the cool surface of the sealed vial. It might not be much, but it was all he had for now.

With a swift motion, Ilrid drew back his arm, preparing to unleash Ember Lash. The creature paused, its eyes narrowing as if sensing the impending danger. This was it—the moment he'd been training for, even if only in theory.

"Ember Lash!" Ilrid shouted, focusing all his energy into the incantation. A streak of fire shot from his hand, striking the creature with a crackling impact. It recoiled, hissing and spluttering as the flames licked at its form.

Maro gave him a thumbs-up from across the water, a grin spreading across his face. "Nice one, Ilrid. Let's keep going."

Ilrid nodded, feeling a surge of confidence. The Drowned Steps held many secrets, but he was ready to uncover them—one Ember Lash at a time.
