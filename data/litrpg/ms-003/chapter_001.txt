The damp air of the Cistern Gate clung to Rhys like a second skin, the dim light from his tin lantern casting eerie shadows on the moss-covered stones. The place had an ancient feel, as if it were holding its breath, waiting for something—or someone—to disturb the silence. 

A figure stepped out from the darkness, his presence heralded by the rhythmic tapping of a wooden stick against the cobblestones. "Tallyman," he announced, his voice echoing slightly in the hollow space. Rhys recognized the role immediately—a keeper of records and overseer of passage between realms.

"Name's Rhys," he replied, holding up the lantern to better see the Tallyman’s features. The man was tall, with a lean build and eyes that seemed to pierce through the gloom, assessing Rhys with an intensity that made him slightly uncomfortable. 

The Tallyman nodded, a small smile playing on his lips. "I see you're carrying your lantern. Good choice. It’s not just about light; it's about showing intent." He paused, tapping his stick twice. "What brings you to the Cistern Gate?" 

Rhys swallowed, feeling the weight of his decision. "I'm here to test my skills," he said, trying to sound more confident than he felt. The Tallyman’s gaze sharpened, and Rhys knew this was his moment to prove himself.

"Very well," the Tallyman replied, tapping his stick thrice in a clear rhythm. "Begin with Mind Spike." 

Rhys nodded, focusing on the mental discipline required for his skill. He closed his eyes, feeling the energy coalesce within him, ready to project his intent into the minds of those who dared challenge him. The Tallyman's presence was a reminder that this was more than just an exercise—it was a test of will and intellect, one he intended to pass with flying colors.
