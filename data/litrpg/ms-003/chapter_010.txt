The air in Fenmouth was thick with the scent of damp earth and ancient secrets. Rhys stood before Warden Ott, his mentor and guide through this strange world where magic and reality intertwined. The warden's eyes, sharp as a hawk’s, scanned Rhys for any sign of hesitation.

"Rhys," Warden Ott began, his voice echoing slightly in the stone chamber, "today you ascend to Level 4. It’s time you learned Stone Ward."

Rhys nodded, feeling a mix of excitement and apprehension. He'd heard stories about this skill—how it could create barriers as solid as the mountains themselves. But he knew that with great power came great responsibility.

"Very well," Warden Ott continued, his voice firm yet encouraging. "Focus your mind. Envision a wall of stone rising from the ground to shield you."

Rhys closed his eyes, taking a deep breath. He could feel the bone charm in his pocket, its presence grounding him. He concentrated on the image of the stone barrier, feeling its weight and texture in his mind.

"Stone Ward," he whispered, the words flowing like a chant. Suddenly, a shimmering wall materialized before him, glowing with an ethereal light. It was solid, unyielding—a testament to his newfound skill.

Warden Ott nodded approvingly. "Well done. Remember, Rhys, Stone Ward is not just about defense. It’s about knowing when to protect and when to let the world in."

Rhys felt a surge of pride. He had earned this level through hard work and determination. As he opened his eyes, he knew that with Stone Ward, he was ready for whatever challenges lay ahead in Fenmouth.

"Thank you, Warden Ott," Rhys said, bowing slightly.

The warden smiled, placing a hand on Rhys's shoulder. "Now, let’s see what else we can uncover together."

With renewed confidence, Rhys stepped forward, ready to explore the mysteries of this magical world.
