The cobblestone streets of Highcut were dimly lit by flickering gas lamps, casting long shadows that danced along the edges of Rhys's vision. He gripped his tin lantern tightly, its warm glow a comforting presence in the cool night air. The lantern wasn't just for light; it was a reminder of home—a small beacon against the unknown.

Ahead, Maro stood waiting, his silhouette outlined by the light from a nearby window. Rhys approached cautiously, aware that every step could lead to new challenges or allies in this strange world. Maro's eyes were sharp, assessing Rhys with an intensity that made him feel like he was under scrutiny.

"Rhys," Maro greeted, his voice carrying a note of curiosity. "I've heard about your skills. Mind Spike and Glass Song, right?"

Rhys nodded, feeling a mix of pride and nerves. "Yeah, those are my tricks."

Maro chuckled softly, the sound echoing slightly in the quiet street. "Well, you're going to need them. The path ahead isn't easy, but I think you're up for it."

Rhys felt a surge of determination. He had trained for this moment, honing his skills in the hopes of making something of himself in Highcut. "I'm ready," he said, his voice steady.

Maro's expression softened, and he extended a hand. "Then let's get started. There's much to learn, and I can help you along the way."

As Rhys shook Maro's hand, he felt a sense of purpose solidify within him. This was just the beginning, and with his skills and a little guidance, he knew he could navigate whatever Highcut threw at him.
