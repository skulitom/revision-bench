The Underbridge was a shadowy expanse of damp stone and flickering torchlight. Rhys stood at its entrance, the weight of his iron key heavy in his pocket. The air was thick with the scent of moss and decay, and the distant sound of water echoed through the cavernous space.

Warden Ott approached, his presence as imposing as the towering archway above them. "Rhys," he began, his voice echoing slightly, "you've proven your worth. But this place holds secrets that many have sought to uncover."

Rhys nodded, his mind racing. He knew the risks but felt a pull towards the mysteries hidden beneath the bridge. "I'm ready," he replied, his voice steady.

Ott handed him a worn map. "This will guide you. But be warned, the path is fraught with danger." As Rhys took the map, he felt a strange sensation—a tug at the charm around his neck. It was gone. Panic surged through him, but he forced himself to focus on Ott's instructions.

"Mind Spike," Rhys whispered to himself, preparing to use his skill to probe Ott's mind for any hidden warnings or advice. The world around him seemed to blur as he honed in on the Warden's thoughts.

Ott continued, oblivious to Rhys's mental probing. "You'll need Stone Ward to protect yourself from the traps ahead. And remember, Glass Song can reveal what's hidden."

Rhys nodded, absorbing the information. He knew he had to rely on his skills and wits. With a deep breath, he stepped into the Underbridge, the iron key now a symbol of the journey ahead. The path was uncertain, but Rhys was determined to uncover the secrets that lay beneath.
