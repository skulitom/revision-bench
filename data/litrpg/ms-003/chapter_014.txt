The Ninth Terrace stretched out before Rhys, its stone pathways winding like ancient veins across the landscape. The air was thick with the scent of moss and old magic, a constant reminder of the world's hidden layers. Maro stood beside him, an enigmatic figure whose presence was as comforting as it was mysterious.

"Rhys," Maro said, his voice carrying the weight of unspoken knowledge, "this place holds more than just secrets. It holds keys."

Rhys nodded, feeling the familiar pull of curiosity in his chest. His hand instinctively reached for the iron key in his pocket, its cold metal a reassuring presence. He glanced at the brass compass he'd recently acquired, its needle spinning slowly as if searching for direction.

"Mind Spike," Rhys murmured, focusing his thoughts. The skill was a subtle one, a way to probe the minds of others and uncover hidden truths. He directed it towards Maro, seeking guidance without words.

Maro's eyes flickered with understanding, a silent acknowledgment of Rhys's intent. "The compass," he said, gesturing towards the artifact in Rhys's hand. "It's more than just a tool for navigation."

Rhys felt a surge of excitement. The compass had always seemed to hum with potential, its purpose not yet fully revealed. He held it up to the light, watching as the needle steadied, pointing towards an unseen force.

"Glass Song," Rhys whispered, activating the skill that allowed him to perceive the hidden energies around him. The world shimmered briefly, revealing faint trails of magic weaving through the air like threads of light.

Following the path illuminated by Glass Song, Rhys and Maro moved forward, their steps silent thanks to Silent Palm. The terrain was uneven, but Quickstep kept them agile, navigating the obstacles with ease.

As they approached a towering archway, Rhys felt a sense of anticipation. The iron key in his pocket seemed to pulse with energy, as if it were eager to unlock whatever lay beyond.

Maro placed a hand on Rhys's shoulder, offering silent encouragement. "Remember, Rhys," he said softly, "every key opens more than just doors."

With that, Rhys took a deep breath, ready to face whatever awaited them on the other side of the archway. The Ninth Terrace was full of mysteries, and he was determined to uncover them all.
