The Sallow Wood was a place of shadows and whispers, where the trees seemed to lean in close as if sharing secrets. Rhys moved cautiously through the underbrush, his tin lantern casting a dim circle of light on the forest floor. The air was thick with an earthy scent, and the occasional rustle of leaves sent a chill down his spine.

Ahead, Sister Elspeth stood by a small clearing, her hands clasped together in silent prayer. Her presence was like a beacon, drawing Rhys forward despite the unease that gnawed at him. "Rhys," she called softly, her voice carrying over the rustling leaves. "Come closer."

As he approached, she turned to face him, her eyes filled with an earnest intensity. "You are ready," she said, nodding towards the ancient stone altar in the center of the clearing. "The time has come for you to learn the Glass Song."

Rhys hesitated, his mind swirling with questions. But Sister Elspeth's gaze was steady, and he knew she meant what she said. Taking a deep breath, he stepped forward, placing his tin lantern on the ground beside him.

Sister Elspeth began to chant, her voice weaving through the air like a delicate thread. Rhys felt a strange energy building within him, a resonance that seemed to harmonize with the very fabric of the forest. He closed his eyes, focusing on the sensation, letting it guide him.

As the final notes of the chant faded, Rhys opened his eyes, feeling a profound shift within himself. The Glass Song was now part of him, a new skill etched into his being. A sense of accomplishment washed over him, mingling with the knowledge that he had just reached level 2.

Sister Elspeth smiled, her eyes twinkling with pride. "You have done well, Rhys. Remember, the Glass Song is both a gift and a responsibility."

Rhys nodded, feeling a newfound confidence. With the Glass Song at his command, he knew he was ready for whatever lay ahead in the Sallow Wood.
