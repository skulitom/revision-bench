The Drowned Steps were eerily silent, the air thick with the scent of brine and decay. The water lapped at Rhys's boots, each step a cautious dance over slippery stones. His tin lantern cast flickering shadows on the moss-covered walls, revealing glimpses of ancient carvings that seemed to whisper secrets in the dim light.

Rhys paused, his hand instinctively reaching for the bone charm in his pocket—a token from his last encounter, its origin as mysterious as its purpose. He felt a tingle of energy, a reminder of the latent power he was learning to wield.

Ahead, Bex moved with a fluid grace that belied her own uncertainty. Her eyes were sharp, scanning the environment for any sign of danger or opportunity. "Rhys," she called softly, her voice barely above the sound of the water. "I think I found something."

Rhys approached cautiously, his lantern illuminating a small alcove in the wall. Nestled within was another bone charm, its surface etched with intricate patterns that seemed to pulse with a faint glow.

"Looks like we're not alone," Bex murmured, her gaze fixed on the new artifact. "These charms... they're more than just trinkets."

Rhys nodded, his mind racing with possibilities. He reached out, fingers hovering over the charm. With a deep breath, he channeled his intellect, focusing on the patterns etched into its surface. The air around them hummed with energy as he activated Glass Song, the vibrations resonating through the water and stone.

The charm responded, its glow intensifying before settling into a steady rhythm. Rhys felt a surge of understanding, a connection to something ancient and powerful. He pocketed the new bone charm, feeling its weight and potential against his skin.

"Let's keep moving," Bex said, her voice laced with determination. "There's more to uncover here."

Together, they stepped forward, the Drowned Steps echoing with the promise of discovery and the thrill of the unknown.
