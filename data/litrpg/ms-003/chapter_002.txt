The narrow cobblestone streets of Highcut were dimly lit, casting long shadows that seemed to dance with the flicker of Rhys's tin lantern. The air was thick with the scent of rain-soaked earth and distant wood smoke. He adjusted the weight of the lantern in his hand, feeling its familiar heft as a comforting presence amidst the uncertainty of this new world.

Ahead, the imposing stone walls of the monastery loomed, their ancient stones whispering stories of centuries past. Sister Elspeth stood at the entrance, her gaze both inviting and assessing. Her habit swished softly with each step, and her eyes held a depth that Rhys found both intriguing and intimidating.

"Welcome, Rhys," she said, her voice carrying an echo of authority. "I trust you've brought your lantern? We'll need it tonight."

Rhys nodded, offering a small smile. "Of course, Sister Elspeth. I'm ready to learn whatever you have in store."

She gestured toward the monastery's entrance, and Rhys followed, his mind already racing with possibilities. The Mind Spike skill was his only known ability, and he wondered how it would come into play here. He had a feeling this night would be anything but ordinary.

As they walked deeper into the monastery, Rhys couldn't shake the sense that he was on the cusp of something monumental. The lantern's glow illuminated the path ahead, casting light on ancient symbols etched into the walls—symbols that seemed to pulse with an energy he could almost feel resonating within him.

Sister Elspeth paused before a large wooden door, her hand resting lightly on its surface. "This is where your journey truly begins," she said, her voice low and mysterious.

Rhys felt a surge of anticipation. He was ready. With the tin lantern as his guide, he stepped forward into the unknown, prepared to unlock the secrets that lay beyond.
