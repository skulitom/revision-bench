The damp air of the Cistern Gate clung to Rhys like a second skin as he stood face-to-face with Maro. The towering gate loomed ahead, its massive stone doors sealed shut, but Rhys knew there was more to this place than met the eye.

"Rhys," Maro's voice echoed through the chamber, "you've come far, but to proceed, you'll need to unlock your potential."

Rhys nodded, his mind already racing through his known skills. Mind Spike and Glass Song could be useful, but something told him he needed more than raw power or precision here.

Maro gestured towards a narrow path leading deeper into the cistern. "The way forward lies in mastering the art of Quickstep," he said, his eyes twinkling with an unspoken challenge.

Rhys took a deep breath, feeling the familiar surge of determination. He closed his eyes, focusing on his agility and intellect, channeling his energy into learning this new skill. As he opened his eyes, he felt a shift within himself—a newfound fluidity in his movements.

"Try it," Maro encouraged, stepping aside to give Rhys space.

With a confident nod, Rhys stepped forward, feeling the ground beneath him shift and adapt to his every move. He could sense the subtle changes in terrain, navigating them with ease as if he were part of the very earth itself.

The iron key in his pocket felt heavier than before, almost as if it were resonating with his newfound agility. Rhys smiled, knowing that this was just the beginning. With Quickstep mastered, he was ready to face whatever lay beyond the Cistern Gate.

As he moved forward, the stone walls seemed to part slightly, revealing a sliver of light beyond. Rhys knew that with each step, he was not only unlocking new paths but also unlocking his true potential.
