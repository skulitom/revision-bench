The air in Highcut was thick with the scent of ancient stone and whispered secrets. Sorrel stood at the threshold of the abbey, her heart pounding like a drumbeat against her ribs. She adjusted the grey cloak around her shoulders, feeling its familiar weight settle into place. 

Sister Elspeth awaited her inside, her presence both imposing and comforting. Sorrel had heard tales of the sister's wisdom, stories that painted her as a guardian of hidden knowledge. Now, standing in her presence, Sorrel felt a mix of awe and determination.

"Welcome, Sorrel," Sister Elspeth said, her voice echoing slightly in the stone hall. "You've come seeking guidance."

Sorrel nodded, her mind racing with questions. "I need to learn more about Thorn Coil. I know it's my only skill right now, but I feel like there's so much more to it."

Sister Elspeth smiled gently, her eyes crinkling at the corners. "Every skill has layers, Sorrel. It begins with understanding its core."

She led Sorrel into a small chamber filled with scrolls and books, their spines worn from use. The air was heavy with the scent of ink and parchment.

"Thorn Coil is more than just a defensive maneuver," Sister Elspeth began, pulling a scroll from the shelf. "It's about channeling your energy, focusing your intent."

Sorrel listened intently, absorbing every word. She could feel the potential within her, waiting to be unlocked.

"Practice it daily," Sister Elspeth advised. "Visualize the thorns wrapping around you, protecting you from harm. Let your mind guide them."

Sorrel nodded, feeling a surge of confidence. She knew this was just the beginning, but with Thorn Coil and Sister Elspeth's guidance, she felt ready to face whatever lay ahead in Highcut.

As they left the chamber, Sorrel couldn't help but feel a sense of purpose. The path ahead was uncertain, but for the first time, she felt equipped to walk it.
