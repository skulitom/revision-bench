The Ninth Terrace was a place of stark contrasts—towering stone walls that shimmered with ancient runes, and a floor that seemed to pulse with an ethereal glow. Sorrel stood at the edge, her breath visible in the chilly air. The Tallyman, a figure cloaked in shadows, approached with the slow, deliberate steps of one who had seen centuries pass.

"Welcome, Sorrel," he intoned, his voice echoing slightly as if resonating from the very stones beneath them. "You've reached Level 5."

Sorrel nodded, her eyes scanning the Tallyman's form. He was an enigma, a keeper of knowledge and balance in this world of numbers and stats. "What's next?" she asked, her voice steady despite the fluttering anticipation in her chest.

The Tallyman extended a hand, palm up, revealing a glowing sigil that danced like living fire. "Iron Skin," he said, his voice almost lost in the wind. "A skill to protect and endure."

Sorrel closed her eyes, focusing on the sigil. She could feel the energy coursing through her veins, wrapping around her like a second skin. It was as if an invisible shield enveloped her, imbuing her with a sense of invincibility.

With a deep breath, she opened her eyes, the sigil now a part of her. "Iron Skin," she whispered, feeling the newfound resilience coursing through her. The Tallyman nodded, his expression unreadable.

"Remember, Sorrel," he said, his voice softer now, "strength is not just in numbers. It's in knowing when to endure and when to strike."

Sorrel nodded, understanding the weight of his words. She adjusted her grey cloak, feeling the copper ring on her finger—a reminder of her journey and the skills she'd honed. The Ninth Terrace was behind her now, but the path ahead was clearer, and she was ready.

As the Tallyman faded back into the shadows, Sorrel turned, her steps confident. The world of numbers and stats awaited, and she was ready to face whatever came next.
