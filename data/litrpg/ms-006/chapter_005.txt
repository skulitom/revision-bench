The Ninth Terrace was a place of mist and mystery, where the air itself seemed to hum with ancient secrets. Sorrel adjusted her grey cloak, feeling the familiar weight of the brass compass in her pocket. It had been a while since she'd acquired it, but its presence was reassuring, a guide through the unpredictable terrain.

Ahead, Nel stood by a cluster of gnarled trees, her eyes scanning the surroundings with practiced ease. "You sure you're ready for this?" she asked, her voice carrying a hint of concern.

Sorrel nodded, though she couldn't shake the flutter of nerves in her stomach. "I've got Thorn Coil and Mind Spike," she reminded herself, trying to sound confident. "I can handle whatever comes our way."

Nel chuckled softly, shaking her head. "You're not the only one with skills here, you know." She gestured toward a path that wound through the trees, disappearing into the mist. "Let's see if your compass can guide us right."

As they stepped forward, Sorrel felt the familiar tingle of anticipation. The Ninth Terrace was known for its illusions and traps, but she had a knack for seeing through them. She activated her brass compass, watching as it spun briefly before settling on a direction.

"Ready?" Nel asked again, this time with a grin.

Sorrel nodded, taking a deep breath. "Let's do this."

They moved forward, the compass leading them deeper into the Terrace. Sorrel kept her senses sharp, ready to deploy Thorn Coil at the first sign of danger or Mind Spike if they encountered any clever foes. The path was treacherous, but with Nel by her side and her trusty compass in hand, she felt prepared for whatever lay ahead.

As they ventured deeper, the mist thickened, wrapping around them like a shroud. Sorrel's heart raced, but she pushed forward, determined to uncover the secrets of the Ninth Terrace.
