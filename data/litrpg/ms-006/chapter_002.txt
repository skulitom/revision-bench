The air was thick with the scent of brine and decay as Sorrel stood at the edge of the Drowned Steps, a decrepit staircase that led into the murky depths below. The water lapped at the crumbling stones, whispering secrets of those who had dared to descend before her. Her heart pounded in her chest like a war drum, but she knew there was no turning back now.

Bex stood beside her, his eyes scanning the shadows beneath the water's surface. "You sure you're ready for this?" he asked, his voice betraying a hint of concern.

Sorrel nodded, pulling the grey cloak tighter around her shoulders. "I've got to try," she replied, her voice steady despite the flutter in her stomach. "If I don't, who will?"

Bex chuckled softly, though there was an edge of sincerity in his laughter. "You're braver than you look, kid."

Sorrel took a deep breath, feeling the familiar tingle of anticipation that always preceded using her Thorn Coil. She closed her eyes, focusing on the skill she had honed since discovering it. The sensation of thorny vines coiling around her fingers was both exhilarating and terrifying.

"Alright," she said, opening her eyes and meeting Bex's gaze. "Let's do this."

With a nod, Bex stepped forward, leading the way down the steps. Sorrel followed closely behind, her mind alert for any sign of danger. The further they descended, the darker it became, until the only light came from the faint glow of bioluminescent algae clinging to the walls.

Suddenly, a low growl echoed through the water, sending chills down Sorrel's spine. She tightened her grip on the thorny vines, ready to defend herself if necessary. But before she could react, Bex was already in motion, his hand reaching out to steady her as they both ducked behind a jagged rock.

"Looks like we're not alone," he whispered, his eyes scanning the shadows for the source of the growl.

Sorrel nodded, feeling the adrenaline surge through her veins. She knew this was just the beginning, but she was ready. With Bex by her side and her Thorn Coil at the ready, she was prepared to face whatever lay in wait beneath the Drowned Steps.
