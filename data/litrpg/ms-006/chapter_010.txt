The Ninth Terrace was a place of both beauty and danger, its lush greenery shadowed by towering stone walls. Sorrel moved with purpose, her agility allowing her to navigate the terrain with ease. The air was thick with the scent of moss and earth, a constant reminder of the magic that permeated this world.

Ahead, Warden Ott stood guard, his presence both reassuring and intimidating. "You're close," he said, nodding towards a shimmering portal at the terrace's edge. Sorrel nodded back, her mind already racing with plans for her next move.

She reached into her inventory, fingers brushing against the cool metal of her copper ring. It was a reminder of her past, a symbol of the power she had yet to fully understand. With a deep breath, she focused on the task at hand.

The portal pulsed with energy, its light flickering like a distant star. Sorrel knew this was her chance to level up, to unlock the potential within her. She closed her eyes, feeling the familiar tingle of magic coursing through her veins.

"Ember Lash," she whispered, drawing on the knowledge she had gleaned from ancient texts and mentors alike. The skill felt foreign yet exhilarating, a new extension of her abilities. As she opened her eyes, the world around her seemed to shift, colors more vibrant, sounds more distinct.

With a flick of her wrist, Sorrel unleashed Ember Lash, a fiery whip of energy that crackled through the air. The portal responded, its light intensifying as if recognizing her newfound power. A surge of warmth spread through her body, and she felt herself grow stronger, more confident.

Level 4. It was official now. Sorrel had reached a new milestone in her journey, her skills expanding with each challenge she faced. She turned to Warden Ott, a smile playing on her lips. "Thank you," she said, gratitude and determination shining in her eyes.

Ott nodded, a rare smile breaking across his weathered face. "You're ready," he replied, his voice filled with pride. Sorrel took a deep breath, knowing that the path ahead was uncertain but filled with endless possibilities.
