Chapter 9

  Name: Sorrel  
  Level: 3  
  Strength: 11

  Skills: Thorn Coil, Mind Spike, Blood Ledger  
  Inventory: grey cloak, brass compass, copper ring  

The damp air of Underbridge clung to Sorrel's skin as she navigated the narrow passageway, her brass compass in hand. The flickering torchlight cast eerie shadows on the moss-covered stones, making the path ahead seem both inviting and foreboding. 

Ahead, Maro stood with a slight hunch, his eyes flickering with an unreadable intent. "Sorrel," he called out, his voice echoing slightly off the walls. "I have something for you."

Curious, Sorrel approached, her cloak swishing softly against the cobblestones. "What's that?" she asked, her tone casual yet tinged with anticipation.

Maro reached into his tattered satchel and pulled out a gleaming copper ring. Its intricate design caught the light, making it seem almost alive. "This," he said, holding it out to her, "is yours. It might come in handy."

Sorrel took the ring, examining it closely. The craftsmanship was exquisite, unlike anything she'd seen before. "Thanks, Maro. How did you get this?"

Maro shrugged, a small smile playing on his lips. "Let's just say I have my sources." He glanced around as if expecting someone or something to appear.

Sensing the tension, Sorrel decided to use one of her skills. She closed her eyes briefly and cast Blood Ledger, allowing her to sense any lingering traces of magic or intent in the air. The skill revealed a faint aura of protection surrounding the ring—a safeguard against dark magics.

"Interesting," she murmured, slipping the ring onto her finger. It fit perfectly, as if it had been waiting for her all along.

Maro nodded approvingly. "Good choice. You'll need it."

As Sorrel turned to continue on her path, she felt a newfound confidence. The Underbridge might be full of unknowns, but with this copper ring and her skills, she was ready for whatever came next.
