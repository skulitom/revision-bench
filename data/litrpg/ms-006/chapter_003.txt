The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across the cobblestone streets of Highcut. Sorrel adjusted the grey cloak around her shoulders, feeling the familiar weight of it settle comfortably against her back. The air was crisp with the scent of autumn leaves and the distant hum of the marketplace began to swell. She had been wandering these streets for what felt like hours, but she knew there was still much to learn before she could call herself a true adventurer.

A figure stepped out from the shadows of an alleyway, his presence commanding even in the dimming light. Warden Ott, a man whose reputation preceded him as much as his imposing stature. Sorrel's heart skipped a beat, but she held her ground, her hands instinctively reaching for the familiar coil of thorns at her side.

"Ah, Sorrel," Ott's voice rumbled like distant thunder. "I've been expecting you." His eyes were sharp, assessing, yet there was a hint of something else—perhaps curiosity or even amusement.

"I'm here to learn," Sorrel replied, her voice steady despite the flutter in her stomach. "To understand what it means to wield Thorn Coil."

Ott nodded, a slow, deliberate motion. "Then you've come to the right place. But remember, knowledge is as much about knowing when to use your skills as it is about mastering them."

Sorrel nodded, taking in his words. She knew that her journey was just beginning, and with each step, she would grow stronger, more agile, and wiser. For now, she had a mentor in Warden Ott, and a world of possibilities stretching out before her like the endless roads of Highcut.

As they walked together through the bustling streets, Sorrel felt a sense of purpose settle over her. She was ready to embrace whatever challenges lay ahead, armed with nothing but her wits, her cloak, and the thorns at her command.
