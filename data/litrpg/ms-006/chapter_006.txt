The bustling streets of Kellsmarket were alive with the usual blend of merchants and adventurers. Sorrel, clad in a nondescript grey cloak, navigated through the crowd with an air of purpose. The brass compass in her pocket felt reassuringly heavy against her thigh—a constant reminder of her quest.

Bex, a fellow adventurer Sorrel had met earlier at the local tavern, caught up to her. "Sorrel, you ready for this?" Bex asked, her eyes scanning the crowded market stalls. "We need to find the merchant with the enchanted amulets before they close."

Sorrel nodded, her mind already racing through potential strategies. "Let's split up. I'll head east, you go west. We'll regroup in ten minutes." She adjusted the compass in her pocket, ensuring it was securely fastened.

As she moved through the throng, Sorrel spotted a suspicious-looking figure darting between stalls. Her instincts kicked in, and she decided to use Thorn Coil. With a swift gesture, thorny vines sprouted from the ground, ensnaring the figure's feet. The man stumbled, caught off guard, giving Sorrel just enough time to cast Mind Spike. A burst of mental energy surged forth, momentarily disorienting him.

"Gotcha!" Sorrel smirked as the man dropped a small, ornate box. She approached cautiously, her hand hovering over the compass. As she reached out, Bex appeared beside her, holding up a similar box. "I found one too!"

Together, they examined their finds. The boxes were identical, each containing an amulet that shimmered with a faint magical aura. Sorrel's heart raced with excitement. They had succeeded.

"Time to regroup and figure out what we've got," Bex said, her voice tinged with anticipation. Sorrel nodded, the thrill of adventure coursing through her veins. Kellsmarket held many secrets, and she was just getting started.
