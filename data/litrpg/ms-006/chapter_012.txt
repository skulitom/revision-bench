The Drowned Steps were a sight to behold, even in their decrepit state. The air was thick with the scent of mildew and ancient stone, while the sound of water trickling from unseen crevices echoed softly around Sorrel. She adjusted her grey cloak, feeling the familiar weight of her copper ring against her skin—a small but comforting reminder of past victories.

Maro stood beside her, his eyes scanning the steps with a mix of curiosity and apprehension. "You sure this is the way?" he asked, his voice barely above a whisper, as if afraid to disturb the silence.

Sorrel nodded, her mind already strategizing their next move. "The legends say it's here," she replied, her voice steady. "We need to be careful. This place isn't just old; it's alive in its own way."

As they descended, the steps seemed to shift slightly beneath them, as if testing their resolve. Sorrel reached for her copper ring, feeling its warmth against her skin. It was a talisman of sorts, a reminder of the power she wielded.

Maro hesitated, looking back at the path they'd come. "What if we're wrong?" he asked, his voice tinged with doubt.

Sorrel smiled, though it didn't quite reach her eyes. "Then we'll find another way," she said confidently. "But for now, let's trust in what we know."

She extended a hand to Maro, offering support as they continued their descent. The Drowned Steps were treacherous, but Sorrel was ready. With her skills—Thorn Coil, Mind Spike, Blood Ledger, and Ember Lash—she felt prepared for whatever lay ahead.

As they moved deeper into the shadows, Sorrel's mind raced with possibilities. Each step brought them closer to their goal, and she knew that with her abilities and Maro by her side, they could overcome any obstacle.

The journey was far from over, but Sorrel was determined. With every challenge, she grew stronger, ready to face whatever the Drowned Steps had in store.
