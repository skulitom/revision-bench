The damp air of Underbridge clung to Sorrel's skin as she moved silently through the shadows, her grey cloak blending seamlessly with the dim light. The compass in her pocket felt reassuringly solid against her thigh, its needle pointing unwaveringly northward. Her mission was clear: gather intel on Warden Ott's movements without raising suspicion.

Warden Ott stood at the far end of the bridge, his back to Sorrel. His imposing figure was a stark contrast against the flickering torchlight, and his eyes scanned the surroundings with practiced vigilance. Sorrel knew she had to be careful; Ott was known for his keen perception and ruthless efficiency.

Sorrel decided it was time to put her skills to use. She activated Thorn Coil, feeling the vines of energy wrap around her limbs, enhancing her agility. With a swift movement, she darted forward, her boots barely making a sound on the cobblestones. As she approached Ott, she prepared to deploy Mind Spike, aiming to probe his thoughts for any valuable information.

Closing the distance, Sorrel whispered an incantation, and a subtle shimmer enveloped her hand. She extended it towards Ott, hoping to catch a glimpse of his mental defenses. The Warden's expression remained stoic, but Sorrel felt a slight resistance in her mind—a mental barrier she hadn't anticipated.

Undeterred, she shifted her focus, activating Blood Ledger to analyze the energy signatures around her. The skill painted a vivid picture of recent activity on the bridge, revealing hidden pathways and potential escape routes. Sorrel noted these details mentally, knowing they could prove invaluable later.

With her objectives partially met, Sorrel retreated into the shadows once more, her heart pounding with the thrill of the close encounter. She had gathered enough intel for now, but she knew this was only the beginning. The Underbridge held many secrets, and Sorrel was determined to uncover them all.
