The air in Fenmouth was thick with the scent of damp earth and wildflowers. Sorrel adjusted the grey cloak around her shoulders, feeling its familiar weight as she stepped through the narrow cobblestone streets. The town had always been a haven for wanderers like her, but today it felt different—charged with potential.

Nel, a fellow traveler with a penchant for riddles, caught up to Sorrel near the bustling market square. "You ready to level up?" Nel asked, eyes twinkling with mischief.

Sorrel nodded, her mind already racing through possibilities. "I've been practicing Thorn Coil all week. But I think it's time to unlock something new."

Nel led her to a secluded grove on the outskirts of town, where the trees whispered secrets in the wind. It was here that Sorrel had first discovered her latent abilities. The grove seemed to hum with energy, as if waiting for her.

Taking a deep breath, Sorrel closed her eyes and focused. She could feel the familiar coil of thorns forming at her fingertips, but this time, she pushed deeper. Her mind reached out, seeking the knowledge hidden within the grove's ancient magic.

A surge of light enveloped her, and suddenly, the world shifted. Sorrel felt a new skill awaken within her—a mental spear, sharp and precise. "Mind Spike," she whispered, the name feeling right on her tongue.

Nel clapped her on the back. "Welcome to level 2, Sorrel. Looks like you've got more tricks up your sleeve now."

Sorrel grinned, feeling a newfound confidence. With Thorn Coil and Mind Spike at her disposal, she was ready for whatever Fenmouth—and beyond—had in store.
