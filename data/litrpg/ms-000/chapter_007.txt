The Cistern Gate loomed before Bright, its ancient stones whispering secrets of the ages. Sister Elspeth stood beside him, her eyes reflecting a calm resolve. "This is where we begin," she said, her voice carrying the weight of tradition and hope.

Bright nodded, feeling the familiar thrum of his skills. Glass Song, Mind Spike, and now, Stone Ward—each one a testament to his growing power. He had only just learned Stone Ward, but it felt like an old friend, a protective embrace against unseen threats.

"Remember," Sister Elspeth continued, "the path ahead is fraught with danger. Use your skills wisely." Her words were a gentle reminder of the responsibility he now carried.

Bright took a deep breath, feeling his vitality surge. He unfolded the map in his inventory, tracing the route with a finger. The journey was long, but he was ready.

As they stepped through the gate, Bright felt a shift within him—a subtle elevation in his being. His level had risen to 3, a milestone marked by newfound strength and understanding.

"Stone Ward," he murmured, activating the skill. A shimmering barrier enveloped them, its presence reassuring against the unknown.

Sister Elspeth smiled, her faith in him unwavering. "Go forth, Bright. The world awaits."

With a determined nod, Bright stepped forward, his heart steady and his mind clear. The adventure had only just begun.
