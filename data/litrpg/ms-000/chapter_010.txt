The Sallow Wood was a place of muted greens and whispered secrets. Bright could feel the energy pulsing through the air, like an ancient heartbeat beneath her feet. She glanced at the folded map in her pocket, tracing the route with a fingertip. The path ahead was clear, but the forest hummed with unseen dangers.

Maro, a fellow adventurer with eyes as sharp as his wit, was by her side. "You ready for whatever's out there?" he asked, a smirk playing on his lips.

Bright nodded, her mind already racing through her options. "Always," she replied, feeling the familiar weight of the bone charm in her hand.

They ventured deeper into the wood, the canopy above filtering the sunlight into soft, dappled patterns. Suddenly, a rustling sound caught Bright's attention. Her instincts kicked in, and she drew upon Stone Ward, creating a protective barrier around them. The ground beneath them trembled slightly as something large moved through the underbrush.

"Stay close," Maro whispered, his hand resting lightly on her shoulder.

Bright nodded, her focus unwavering. As they moved forward, she felt a shift in her abilities—a new energy coursing through her veins. It was subtle but unmistakable. She had reached level 4.

A sense of anticipation filled her as she realized what this meant: Quickstep. The skill had been on the horizon for a while, and now it was within reach. With a deep breath, she closed her eyes, channeling her energy into the new ability.

When she opened them again, the world seemed to slow down. She could feel the rhythm of the forest, the ebb and flow of its life force. With a swift motion, she activated Quickstep, her movements fluid and precise. The rustling ceased, and they moved forward with newfound speed.

Maro looked at her in awe. "That was something else," he said, grinning.

Bright smiled back, feeling more confident than ever. The Sallow Wood held many secrets, but she was ready to uncover them all.
