The damp air of the Cistern Gate clung to Bright's skin like a second layer, the ancient stones whispering secrets as she stepped forward. The towering archway loomed above, its surface etched with runes that seemed to pulse in the dim light filtering through the overgrowth. A sense of foreboding settled over her, but she brushed it aside, focusing on the task at hand.

Warden Ott stood before her, his eyes as sharp as the blade he carried, though it was holstered securely at his side. His presence was commanding, yet there was a hint of weariness in his stance that spoke of long years spent guarding this place.

"Welcome, Bright," he greeted, his voice echoing slightly in the cavernous space. "You're here to see the Oracle, I presume?"

Bright nodded, clutching the folded map and bone charm in her pocket. "Yes, Warden Ott. The map led me here, but I need more guidance."

Ott's gaze flickered to the map before returning to her. "The Oracle is a tricky one. She doesn't take kindly to those who can't find their own way."

Bright felt a twinge of nerves but masked it with confidence. "I'm not afraid of a little challenge."

Warden Ott chuckled, a sound that seemed out of place in the somber surroundings. "Good. Just remember, the path isn't always straight. Be ready to adapt."

As she prepared to move forward, Bright felt the familiar weight of her skills at her disposal. Glass Song could shatter illusions, Mind Spike could pierce through deception, and Stone Ward would protect her from unexpected threats. She knew she had what it took to face whatever lay ahead.

With a nod to the warden, she stepped into the shadows beyond the gate, the map guiding her deeper into the unknown. The adventure was just beginning.
