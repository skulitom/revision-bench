The Sallow Wood was a place of whispers, where the trees seemed to murmur secrets as old as time. Bright felt the weight of the folded map in their pocket, its edges worn from countless folds and refolds. They paused, listening to the rustling leaves, trying to discern any hidden message or guidance. The map offered no answers here; it was a silent enigma.

Bex, a wiry figure with eyes like polished amber, approached. "You're new here," they said, voice laced with curiosity and a hint of caution. Bright nodded, feeling the familiar tug of uncertainty. They had ventured into this world seeking something undefined, yet the Sallow Wood seemed to hold more questions than answers.

"Ever heard of Glass Song?" Bex asked, tilting their head as if expecting an answer Bright couldn't possibly know. "It's a rare skill, used by those who can weave magic through song."

Bright nodded again, feeling a flicker of recognition. They had learned about it, yes. The ability to manipulate glass with melody was a powerful tool, one they hoped would be useful in this strange land.

"Care to show me?" Bex challenged, stepping closer. Bright hesitated only for a moment before taking a deep breath. They closed their eyes, letting the rhythm of their heartbeat guide them. A soft hum began, growing into a melody that seemed to dance through the air, weaving around the trees and leaves.

The forest responded, glass shards suspended in mid-air, shimmering like diamonds under the dappled sunlight. Bex watched, eyes wide with amazement. "Impressive," they said, a note of respect threading through their voice.

Bright opened their eyes, feeling a surge of confidence. The Sallow Wood was still full of mysteries, but for now, they had found a way to begin unraveling them.
