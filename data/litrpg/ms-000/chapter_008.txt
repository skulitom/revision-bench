The bustling streets of Kellsmarket were alive with the clatter of carts and the chatter of merchants hawking their wares. Bright navigated through the throng, her senses attuned to the hum of magic that lingered in the air like a persistent mist. She clutched the folded map tightly in her pocket, its edges worn from frequent use.

As she turned a corner, the imposing figure of Warden Ott came into view. His stern gaze swept over the crowd, a silent warning to any troublemakers lurking among the innocent shoppers. Bright approached cautiously, her mind racing with possibilities of what he might need from her.

"Ah, Bright," Ott greeted, his voice gruff but not unkind. "I've been expecting you."

Bright nodded, her curiosity piqued. "What's up, Warden?"

Ott gestured to a nearby stall where an old crone sat surrounded by trinkets and charms. "There's something I need from that woman," he said cryptically.

Bright hesitated for only a moment before stepping forward. The crone regarded her with rheumy eyes, her lips curling into a knowing smile. "You seek the charm of bones, young one?"

Bright nodded, feeling a strange pull towards the item. With a flick of her wrist, she deftly used Glass Song to shatter a nearby glass bottle, drawing the crone's attention. The shards sparkled like diamonds in the sunlight, mesmerizing the old woman.

"Very well," the crone croaked, reaching into a dusty box and pulling out a small, intricately carved bone charm. "Take it, but be warned: it holds more than meets the eye."

Bright accepted the charm with gratitude, tucking it next to her folded map. As she turned to leave, Ott's voice called out, "Remember, Bright, some things are best left unexplored."

She offered a small smile and nodded. With the bone charm now in her possession, Bright felt a new sense of purpose. Kellsmarket was full of secrets, and she was determined to uncover them all.
