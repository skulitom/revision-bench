Chapter 14

[ STATUS ]  
  Name: Bright  
  Level: 5  
  Strength: 11  
  Agility: 8  
  Intellect: 12  
  Vitality: 13  
  Skills: Glass Song, Mind Spike, Stone Ward, Quickstep, Silent Palm  
  Inventory: folded map, copper ring  

---

The Cistern Gate loomed before Bright, its ancient stones whispering secrets of the ages. Warden Ott stood at the entrance, his stern gaze fixed on her. "You must prove your worth to pass," he declared, his voice echoing slightly in the damp air.

Bright nodded, her mind already racing through her options. The folded map in her pocket felt reassuringly solid against her thigh. She took a deep breath and stepped forward, her Quickstep skill ready to be deployed if things went south.

"Alright, Warden," she began, "let's see what you've got."

Ott raised an eyebrow, clearly unimpressed by her casual demeanor. "You'll need more than bravado to get through my gate."

Bright smirked, feeling a surge of confidence. She knew her skills were up to the task. "How about a little Glass Song?" she suggested, her voice lilting as she began to hum.

The air around them shimmered, glass-like tendrils forming from her fingertips. Ott's eyes widened slightly, but he remained composed. "Impressive," he admitted grudgingly, "but not enough."

Bright nodded, understanding the challenge. She needed to combine her skills strategically. She closed her eyes, focusing on the copper ring she wore. Its subtle warmth reminded her of the power it held.

"Mind Spike," she whispered, her intellect channeling through the ring. A sharp, focused beam of energy shot from her mind, aimed at Ott's defenses. He flinched, but his Stone Ward was up in an instant, absorbing the attack.

Bright grinned, knowing she had to be more creative. She shifted her stance, feeling the weight of her Vitality and Agility supporting her next move. "Stone Ward," she called out, raising her hands to form a protective barrier around herself.

The ward shimmered into existence, deflecting any potential counterattacks. Ott nodded in respect, his stern demeanor softening slightly. "You're not as easy to deter as I thought."

Bright's heart raced with excitement. She had him now. With a silent gesture, she activated Silent Palm, her movements becoming almost ghostly. She danced around Ott, her steps light and precise.

"Quickstep," she murmured, her agility allowing her to dart past his defenses effortlessly. The Cistern Gate opened before her, the path beyond inviting and mysterious.

Ott watched in awe as Bright passed through, a knowing smile playing on her lips. "Until next time, Warden," she called back, stepping into the unknown with confidence.
