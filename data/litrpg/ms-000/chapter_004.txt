Bright stood at the edge of Kellsmarket, the vibrant hub of trade and intrigue. The air buzzed with the chatter of merchants and the clinking of coins. He adjusted his grip on the folded map, feeling its familiar texture in his pocket. The Tallyman was here today, a figure shrouded in mystery and whispers.

The Tallyman’s eyes met Bright's, a knowing glint behind his spectacles. "You’ve grown stronger," he said, voice smooth as silk. "But true power lies in understanding the minds of others."

Bright nodded, intrigued. The Tallyman extended a hand, revealing a sealed vial. "This will aid you in learning Mind Spike."

As Bright took the vial, a rush of energy surged through him. He felt his intellect expand, the boundaries of his mind stretching like elastic. The Tallyman’s voice echoed in his head, guiding him through the process.

"Focus," the Tallyman instructed. "Feel the thoughts around you. Connect with them."

Bright closed his eyes, concentrating. Images and voices swirled before him—memories of deals made, secrets exchanged. He reached out with his mind, touching a thought here, nudging it gently there.

A sudden clarity washed over him. He opened his eyes, feeling a newfound confidence. "I did it," he whispered to himself.

The Tallyman smiled, nodding approvingly. "You’ve learned Mind Spike. Use it wisely."

Bright pocketed the vial and unfolded the map, tracing routes with his finger. The market around him seemed different now, alive with possibilities. He was ready for whatever Kellsmarket had in store.

As he turned to leave, a voice called out behind him. "Bright! Over here!"

He spun around, spotting a familiar face waving frantically. A new adventure awaited, and Bright was eager to embrace it.
