Bright stepped into the bustling heart of Kellsmarket, where the air buzzed with the chatter of merchants and the clinking of coins. The cobblestone streets were alive with vibrant stalls, each one a kaleidoscope of colors and scents. Bright's eyes scanned the throng, searching for Sister Elspeth, who was supposed to meet him here. 

The market was a labyrinth of opportunity and distraction, but Bright’s mind was focused. He could hear the faint melody of Glass Song playing in his head, a reminder of the delicate balance between chaos and control. It was a skill he’d honed over time, one that allowed him to weave harmony from discord. As he navigated through the crowd, he slipped into Quickstep, moving with a fluid grace that kept him ahead of the throng.

Sister Elspeth appeared at last, her habit fluttering like a banner in the wind. She beckoned Bright over with a gentle wave, and he approached, the folded map in his pocket feeling heavier than it had moments before. 

“Bright,” she began, her voice carrying the weight of urgency, “there’s been an incident. Your bone charm...” Her words trailed off, concern etched into her features.

Bright’s heart sank. The bone charm was more than a trinket; it was a safeguard, a piece of his past that he carried with him always. He nodded, understanding the gravity of its loss. 

“We need to act quickly,” Elspeth continued. “The market is vast, but we can use your skills to track it down. Mind Spike might help us find any traces left behind.”

Bright nodded again, determination setting in. With Stone Ward ready to protect them from any unforeseen threats, they began their search, moving through the market with purpose. The challenge was daunting, but Bright knew that with Elspeth by his side and his skills at his disposal, he could face whatever came next.
