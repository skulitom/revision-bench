Chapter 3

The Ninth Terrace was a place of ethereal beauty, with towering glass structures that refracted the sunlight into a kaleidoscope of colors. Bright stood at the edge of this mesmerizing landscape, the folded map in his pocket feeling like an anchor to reality amidst the shimmering illusions.

Bex, a fellow traveler with an air of mystery and experience, approached him. Her eyes were sharp, scanning the surroundings as if she could see through the layers of light and glass. "You're new here," she observed, her voice carrying a hint of amusement.

Bright nodded, feeling a mix of excitement and apprehension. "Yeah, just got here. This place is... something else."

Bex chuckled softly. "It's all about finding your way. The Ninth Terrace can be tricky." She gestured towards the path ahead, where beams of light danced across the ground like playful spirits.

Bright took a deep breath, feeling the weight of his inventory. The folded map seemed to pulse with potential, and the sealed vial in his pocket was a mystery yet to be unraveled. "I'm trying to master my Glass Song," he admitted, hoping to impress Bex with his dedication.

She raised an eyebrow, intrigued. "Glass Song, huh? That's not something you hear every day. Show me what you've got."

Bright closed his eyes, focusing on the vibrations of the glass around him. He felt the familiar hum in his chest, a resonance that connected him to the very essence of this place. As he opened his eyes, the air shimmered with the song's melody, creating delicate patterns of light.

Impressed, Bex clapped her hands together. "Not bad for a level one. You've got potential." She reached into her cloak and pulled out another sealed vial, handing it to Bright. "This might come in handy. Keep it safe."

Bright took the vial, feeling its weight and wondering about its contents. "Thanks," he said, grateful for the unexpected gift.

Bex nodded, giving him a small smile. "Just remember, the Ninth Terrace is full of surprises. Stay sharp."

With that, she turned and walked deeper into the glass forest, leaving Bright to ponder his next move. The sealed vial in his pocket felt like a key to unlocking new possibilities, and he knew his journey was just beginning.
