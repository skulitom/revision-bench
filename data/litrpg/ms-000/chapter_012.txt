The air in Fenmouth was thick with the scent of damp earth and old stone. Bright adjusted his grip on the folded map, feeling its familiar creases under his fingers. He had been wandering these streets for hours, trying to make sense of Sister Elspeth's cryptic clues.

As he turned a corner, he spotted her standing by an ancient fountain, the water bubbling softly around her feet. Her eyes met his, and she beckoned him closer with a gentle wave.

"Ah, Bright," she greeted, her voice carrying the warmth of a familiar tune. "I see you've been exploring."

Bright nodded, approaching cautiously. "Yes, Sister Elspeth. I'm trying to figure out where to go next."

She smiled, a knowing glint in her eyes. "The path isn't always straight, my friend. Sometimes, it's the detours that lead us to our destination."

Bright considered her words, feeling a sense of calm wash over him. He decided to use his Stone Ward, creating a protective barrier around them as they spoke. It was always wise to be prepared for anything.

"Thank you," he said, feeling the reassuring presence of the ward.

Sister Elspeth reached into her robe and pulled out a small, intricately designed copper ring. "I've been keeping an eye on your progress," she explained. "This ring will aid you in your journey."

Bright took the ring, examining it closely. It felt warm to the touch, as if imbued with its own life force.

"Thank you, Sister Elspeth," he said, slipping the ring onto his finger. "I'll make good use of it."

She nodded, her expression serene. "Remember, Bright, every step you take is a step towards understanding yourself and your place in this world."

With that, she turned and walked away, leaving Bright to ponder her words. He felt a renewed sense of purpose, ready to face whatever challenges lay ahead in Fenmouth.
