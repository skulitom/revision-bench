Chapter 6

[ STATUS ]  
  Name: Bright  
  Level: 2  
  Strength: 10  
  Agility: 8  
  Intellect: 8  
  Vitality: 13  
  Skills: Glass Song, Mind Spike  
  Inventory: folded map  

Bright crouched beneath the Underbridge, the damp air clinging to his skin. The narrow passageway was dimly lit by the occasional flicker of distant streetlights, casting long shadows that danced across the cobblestones. He clutched the folded map tightly in his hand, its creases familiar under his fingers.

A rustling sound caught his attention. "Who's there?" he called out, his voice echoing softly off the stone walls.

A figure stepped into the light—a man with a clipboard and a badge that read "Tallyman." He paused, studying Bright with a calculating gaze. "You must be Bright," he said, his tone neutral. "I'm here to check your progress. You're level 2, right?"

Bright nodded, feeling a mix of anticipation and nerves. "Yeah, that's me. I've been working on my skills."

The Tallyman flipped through his notes, eyes scanning the pages. "Let's see how you fare with Glass Song and Mind Spike," he suggested.

Bright took a deep breath and began to hum softly, the vibrations resonating in his chest. The sound was clear and pure, like a crystal chime. The Tallyman watched, nodding in approval as Bright's skillful control over the melody became evident.

"Good," the Tallyman said, jotting down notes. "Now, Mind Spike."

Bright closed his eyes, focusing his thoughts with precision. He imagined a sharp, crystalline point of energy extending from his mind, piercing through mental barriers. The Tallyman raised an eyebrow, impressed by the clarity and accuracy of Bright's technique.

"Excellent," the Tallyman remarked. "You're progressing well. Keep honing these skills."

As Bright opened his eyes, he felt a surge of confidence. The map in his inventory seemed to pulse with potential, guiding him toward his next challenge. With a nod of thanks, he turned and stepped out from under the bridge, ready for whatever lay ahead.
