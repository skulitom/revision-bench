Bright stood at the edge of Kellsmarket, the bustling hub of commerce and chatter. The air buzzed with the sound of merchants hawking their wares and adventurers sharing tales of distant lands. Bright clutched the folded map tightly in one hand, feeling the rough texture under their fingertips. It was their only guide in this sprawling labyrinth of stalls and vendors.

Nel, a fellow adventurer with a knack for finding trouble, approached with an easy grin. "Bright, my friend! Ready to dive into the heart of Kellsmarket?" Nel's eyes sparkled with mischief as they gestured towards the crowded center.

Bright nodded, feeling a mix of excitement and apprehension. They had only just begun their journey, and this market was a world unto itself. "Yeah, let's see what we can find," Bright replied, adjusting the map in their pocket for easy access.

As they navigated through the throngs of people, Bright felt the familiar hum of energy coursing through them. It was a reminder of the Glass Song skill they possessed—a unique ability to manipulate glass structures with sound. Though they had yet to master it fully, the potential was thrilling.

Nel pointed towards a stall selling intricate glass figurines. "I bet you could do something amazing with those," Nel teased, nudging Bright playfully.

Bright's eyes lit up at the challenge. "Maybe I will," they said, stepping closer to examine the delicate creations. With a deep breath, they closed their eyes, focusing on the vibrations of their own voice. The Glass Song began as a faint whisper, growing stronger with each note.

In that moment, Bright felt a connection to something greater—a promise of what they could become. The market around them faded into the background as they honed their skill, ready to face whatever Kellsmarket had in store.
