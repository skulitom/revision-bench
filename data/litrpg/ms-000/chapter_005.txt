Chapter 5

[ STATUS ]  
  Name: Bright  
  Level: 2  
  Strength: 10

Bright stood at the edge of the Ninth Terrace, the wind carrying whispers of ancient secrets through the rustling leaves. The towering stone steps spiraled down into the misty depths below, a path that seemed to vanish into the unknown. His eyes scanned the horizon, seeking any sign of Maro, his guide in this strange realm.

The folded map in his pocket felt like a lifeline, a tangible piece of reality amidst the surreal landscape. He unfolded it carefully, tracing the delicate lines with a finger. The map was a puzzle, filled with cryptic symbols and landmarks that seemed to shift under his gaze. But one thing was certain: he needed to find Maro.

A sudden rustling from behind made Bright turn sharply. There stood Maro, a figure cloaked in shadows, his eyes gleaming with an otherworldly light. "Bright," he said, his voice echoing slightly in the open air. "You must be careful. The Ninth Terrace is not what it seems."

Bright nodded, feeling a chill run down his spine. "I know," he replied, his voice steady despite the unease gnawing at him. "I need to find the sealed vial. It's crucial."

Maro's expression darkened. "The vial is lost," he said cryptically. "But perhaps it is not as lost as you think."

Bright furrowed his brow, trying to decipher Maro's words. "What do you mean?"

"The Ninth Terrace holds many secrets," Maro continued, stepping closer. "And some of them are hidden in plain sight."

Bright felt a surge of determination. He had to find the vial, no matter what it took. With a deep breath, he closed his eyes and focused on his skills. The Glass Song echoed softly in his mind, a melody that could reveal hidden truths.

As the notes danced through the air, Bright's senses sharpened. He could feel the presence of something nearby, something elusive yet tangible. With a quick flick of his wrist, he unleashed a Mind Spike, sending a probing thought into the ether.

The world around him seemed to shift, revealing glimpses of hidden paths and concealed objects. And there, nestled among the roots of an ancient tree, was the sealed vial, glowing faintly with an inner light.

Bright reached out, his fingers brushing against the cool glass. But as he grasped it, the vial slipped through his fingers, disappearing into the shadows once more.

Maro's voice broke through his frustration. "The Ninth Terrace is a test, Bright. You must learn to see beyond what is visible."

Bright clenched his fists, determination burning in his chest. He would find the vial, and he would pass this test. With renewed resolve, he turned back to the map, ready to uncover the secrets of the Ninth Terrace.
