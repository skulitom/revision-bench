Sorrel stood at the edge of Kellsmarket, the bustling square alive with merchants and adventurers alike. The scent of roasted chestnuts mingled with the sharp tang of magic in the air. Today was no ordinary day; she had a mission to complete, and time was slipping through her fingers like sand.

The Warden Ott, a towering figure with eyes as keen as an eagle's, approached her. "Sorrel," he said, his voice a deep rumble, "I need you to find the sealed vial. It's crucial to our plans."

Sorrel nodded, feeling the weight of responsibility settle on her shoulders. The vial was lost somewhere in this chaotic market, and she had only her skills and wits to rely on.

She activated Quickstep, her feet barely touching the ground as she weaved through the crowd with ease. Her agility served her well, allowing her to dodge a careening cart and a group of boisterous children playing tag. The market was a labyrinth of stalls and people, but she moved like a shadow, unnoticed and swift.

Her mind raced, trying to piece together where the vial could be. She had heard rumors of a shady dealer in potions lurking near the eastern end of the market. With that clue in mind, she pressed on, her heart pounding in rhythm with her steps.

As she neared the dealer's stall, a sudden commotion erupted nearby. A thief was making off with a merchant's goods, and the crowd surged forward, drawn by the spectacle. Sorrel hesitated for a moment, but then she knew what to do. She activated Frost Nail, sending a shard of ice flying towards the thief. The ice struck true, pinning him in place and drawing the attention of nearby guards.

With the thief apprehended, Sorrel slipped away unnoticed, her mind back on the task at hand. The dealer's stall was just ahead, and she could feel the answer to her quest within reach. She took a deep breath, ready to uncover the secrets hidden in Kellsmarket.
