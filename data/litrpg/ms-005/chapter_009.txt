The air in the Sallow Wood was thick with the scent of damp earth and decaying leaves. Sorrel's boots crunched softly on the forest floor as she moved cautiously through the underbrush. The trees loomed overhead like silent sentinels, their branches intertwining to form a canopy that barely let any light through.

Maro, a burly figure with furrowed brows, was pacing back and forth, his hands clenching and unclenching at his sides. "We've been here for hours," he grumbled, eyes darting around nervously. "If they're not here by sundown, I'm calling it."

Sorrel nodded, her mind racing. She needed to find the hidden entrance to the underground lair before darkness fell. Taking a deep breath, she activated Echo Sight, her vision shifting slightly as she scanned the area for any signs of movement or hidden paths.

A faint outline appeared in the corner of her vision—a narrow tunnel obscured by thick vines and foliage. "I found something," she called out, moving towards it with purpose.

Maro's eyes widened as he saw the path Sorrel had discovered. "Good work, Sorrel. Let's move."

As they approached the entrance, Sorrel felt a shiver run down her spine. The air grew colder, and she could sense the presence of something watching them from the shadows. Without hesitation, she reached into her inventory and pulled out the salt pouch, scattering its contents around the entrance as a protective measure.

"Stay alert," she warned Maro, stepping forward with Quickstep activated. Her movements were swift and silent, each step calculated to avoid any traps or ambushes that might lie ahead.

The tunnel beckoned them deeper into the unknown, and Sorrel knew that whatever awaited them in the darkness would test their resolve. But for now, they had found their way, and that was enough.
