The bustling Kellsmarket was alive with the hum of commerce and chatter. Sorrel navigated the crowded stalls with practiced ease, her eyes darting from one vendor to another. The scent of spices mingled with the aroma of freshly baked bread, creating a heady mix that made her stomach rumble.

Bex, her mentor in this strange world of magic and markets, was already waiting by a small stall draped in shimmering cloth. "You're late," Bex chided softly, though there was no real annoyance in her voice.

Sorrel shrugged nonchalantly, her quickstep skill allowing her to weave through the throng without breaking stride. "Traffic's always unpredictable here," she replied with a grin.

Bex nodded, motioning for Sorrel to follow as they approached the stall. The vendor, an old man with twinkling eyes, greeted them warmly. "Ah, you must be Sorrel. I've been expecting you."

Sorrel raised an eyebrow, her curiosity piqued. "And why's that?"

The old man chuckled, producing a small, ornate flute from beneath the cloth. "This," he said, holding it up to the light, "is a rare artifact. It holds the power of Glass Song."

Sorrel's heart skipped a beat. She had heard tales of this skill, one that could weave melodies into reality, shaping the world around her. But she had never thought she'd encounter it so soon.

Bex stepped forward, placing a hand on Sorrel's shoulder. "This is your chance to learn it," she said, her voice filled with encouragement.

Sorrel nodded, taking the flute from the vendor's hands. As she held it, a warm sensation spread through her fingertips, and a new skill icon appeared in her mind's eye: Glass Song.

She took a deep breath, feeling a surge of power course through her veins. "Thank you," she said to the vendor, her voice steady despite the excitement bubbling inside her.

As they left the stall, Sorrel felt a shift within herself. She was no longer just a novice; she was a level 4 adventurer with new abilities to explore.

Bex smiled, squeezing Sorrel's shoulder. "Welcome to the next chapter of your journey," she said, and Sorrel couldn't help but smile back. The world was full of possibilities, and she was ready to embrace them all.
