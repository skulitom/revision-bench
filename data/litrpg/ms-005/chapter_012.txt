The morning mist clung to the cobblestones of Fenmouth like a shroud, casting the town in an ethereal glow. Sorrel's boots crunched softly on the damp ground as she made her way through the narrow streets, her brass compass swinging gently from her belt. The familiar scent of salt and sea wafted through the air, mingling with the aroma of freshly baked bread from the nearby bakery.

Sister Elspeth stood at the entrance to the abbey, her dark eyes scanning the horizon. "Good morning, Sorrel," she called out, her voice carrying over the gentle hum of the waking town. "I trust you're ready for today's task?"

Sorrel nodded, her mind already racing through the possibilities. "As always, Sister." She felt a surge of excitement at the thought of what lay ahead. Today was not just another day in Fenmouth; it was an opportunity to prove herself.

The abbey grounds were alive with activity. Monks and novices moved between the buildings, their robes rustling softly. Sorrel's eyes flicked to her inventory: the salt pouch and brass compass. She knew both would be crucial today.

"Let's get started," Sister Elspeth said, her tone firm yet encouraging. "We need to ensure the perimeter is secure before the council meeting this afternoon."

Sorrel took a deep breath, feeling the familiar rush of adrenaline. She activated Echo Sight, her vision expanding to reveal the hidden pathways and potential threats around them. The world seemed to pulse with energy, each sound and movement amplified.

"Lead the way," Sorrel replied, stepping forward with purpose. Her Quickstep skill kicked in effortlessly, allowing her to move swiftly and silently through the grounds. She felt the cool breeze against her skin, the world around her a blur of motion and sound.

As they moved, Sorrel kept an eye on Sister Elspeth, ready to deploy Frost Nail if any danger arose. The abbey was their sanctuary, and she would protect it with every skill at her disposal.

The morning sun began to break through the mist, casting long shadows that danced across the cobblestones. Fenmouth was waking up, and Sorrel was ready to face whatever the day had in store.
