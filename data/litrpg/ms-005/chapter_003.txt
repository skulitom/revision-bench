The air in Fenmouth was thick with the scent of damp earth and wildflowers. Sorrel's boots crunched on the gravel path as they made their way towards the monastery. The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across the landscape. Sister Elspeth stood at the entrance, her habit fluttering slightly in the evening breeze.

"Ah, Sorrel, you're here," she said with a warm smile, gesturing for them to come closer. "I've been expecting you."

Sorrel nodded, feeling a mixture of excitement and nerves. They were about to embark on their first real adventure in this LitRPG world, and Sister Elspeth was the one who would guide them.

"Today, we'll start with something simple," Elspeth continued. "I want you to practice your Quickstep around the cloister. It's a basic skill, but it will help you become more agile and aware of your surroundings."

Sorrel took a deep breath, feeling their heart rate pick up slightly. They knew agility was one of their stronger attributes, but putting it into practice was another story.

"Remember," Elspeth added, "focus on the rhythm and flow. Let your movements be fluid and natural."

With that, Sorrel stepped forward, their mind clearing as they began to move. The cloister's ancient stones seemed to hum beneath their feet, encouraging them to find their pace. Each step was a dance, a delicate balance of speed and grace.

As they circled the cloister, Sorrel felt a sense of freedom they hadn't known before. The world around them blurred into a whirl of colors and sounds, but they remained focused, their movements becoming more confident with each pass.

Sister Elspeth watched from a distance, a gentle smile playing on her lips. "You're doing well," she called out. "Keep it up."

Sorrel nodded, feeling a surge of determination. They were just starting this journey, but already they could sense the potential within them. With each step, they were becoming more than they had ever imagined.
