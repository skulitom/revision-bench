The morning mist clung to the cobblestones of Fenmouth like a shroud as Sorrel made her way through the town. The air was thick with the scent of damp earth and the distant hum of activity. She had been here before, but something felt different today—a sense of purpose that tugged at her core.

Sorrel paused outside an unassuming building with a sign that read "Tallyman's Office." A man in a crisp, gray suit stood by the door, his eyes sharp and assessing. This was the Tallyman, the official who kept track of adventurers like her. She approached him, feeling a mix of anticipation and nerves.

"Name's Sorrel," she said, extending a hand. "I'm here to level up."

The Tallyman nodded, taking her hand in a firm grip. "Level 2, you say? Let's see what you've got." He handed her a small, shimmering orb. "This is your new skill, Frost Nail. Use it wisely."

Sorrel watched as the orb dissolved into her skin, a chill spreading through her veins. She could feel the power of ice coursing through her, waiting to be unleashed. The Tallyman's eyes twinkled with approval.

"Now, show me what you can do." He gestured towards a nearby training dummy, its wooden form swaying gently in the breeze.

Sorrel took a deep breath and focused. With a swift motion, she channeled her newfound ability, sending a shard of ice hurtling towards the dummy. It struck with precision, shattering the wood into splinters. The Tallyman clapped his hands together, impressed.

"Excellent work," he said, handing her a small scroll. "You've earned your level 2 status."

Sorrel felt a surge of pride and excitement. She was ready to take on whatever challenges lay ahead in Fenmouth—and beyond. With her cracked whetstone and sealed vial in hand, she knew she was prepared for the journey ahead.

As she left the Tallyman's office, Sorrel couldn't help but smile. The world was full of possibilities, and she was ready to explore them all.
