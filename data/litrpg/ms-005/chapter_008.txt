The Sallow Wood was a place of whispers and shadows, where the trees seemed to lean in close, eager to share their secrets. Sorrel's boots crunched softly on the fallen leaves as she moved through the underbrush, her heart beating a steady rhythm against her ribs. The Tallyman stood before her, his eyes like polished obsidian, reflecting the muted light filtering through the canopy above.

"Welcome, Sorrel," he intoned, his voice echoing slightly in the quiet of the wood. "You seek the path forward, but first, you must prove your worth."

Sorrel nodded, her mind already racing through her options. She could use Quickstep to gain a better position or Frost Nail to test the Tallyman's defenses. But something about his demeanor suggested caution.

"Very well," the Tallyman continued, stepping aside to reveal a narrow path that disappeared into the dense foliage. "The journey ahead is fraught with peril. Use your skills wisely."

Sorrel took a deep breath and activated Echo Sight, her vision shifting as she scanned the area for hidden threats. The wood seemed to pulse around her, revealing glimpses of movement just beyond her line of sight.

"Something's off," she murmured, noting the subtle shift in the air. "Stay alert."

With a swift motion, she reached into her pouch and sprinkled a pinch of salt on the ground, watching as it settled into the earth. The Tallyman raised an eyebrow but said nothing, allowing her to proceed.

Sorrel stepped forward, her mind focused on the path ahead. She knew that losing the cracked whetstone had been a setback, but she was determined not to let it hinder her progress. As she moved deeper into the Sallow Wood, she felt a surge of determination. This was just the beginning.
