Sorrel stood at the edge of Highcut, the cobblestone streets bustling with merchants and townsfolk. The air was thick with the scent of spices and the distant hum of conversation. Maro, a burly figure with a scar running down his cheek, approached her with an eager grin.

"Hey Sorrel! Heard you got some new skills. Mind showing me what you've got?" Maro asked, his voice a deep rumble that seemed to echo off the buildings.

Sorrel nodded, feeling a mix of excitement and nerves. She took a deep breath, her brass compass clutched tightly in her hand. "Sure thing, Maro. Let's start with Quickstep."

With a swift motion, she activated her Quickstep ability. Her feet moved with incredible speed, darting between the market stalls as if she were a shadow. The crowd gasped, some stepping back to give her space. Sorrel felt the wind rush past her face, exhilarating and freeing.

Maro watched in awe, his eyes wide. "Whoa! You're like lightning!"

Sorrel grinned, slowing down to a walk. "Next up, Frost Nail." She raised her hand, and a small icicle formed at her fingertips. With precision, she aimed it at a nearby crate, shattering it into pieces.

The crowd murmured in amazement as frost lingered in the air. Maro clapped his hands together. "Impressive! What else you got?"

Sorrel nodded, feeling more confident. "Echo Sight." She closed her eyes, focusing on the energy around her. The world seemed to expand, revealing hidden details and pathways. She opened her eyes, seeing the market in a new light.

"See anything interesting?" Maro asked, leaning in closer.

Sorrel shook her head, but she noticed something odd—a slight shimmer near the fountain. "I think there's something here," she said, pointing.

Maro squinted at the spot. "Hmm, let's check it out."

As they approached, Sorrel felt a tug in her pocket. Her salt pouch was gone. She froze, panic rising. "Maro, my salt pouch!"

He turned, his expression serious. "Let's find it before anyone else does."

Sorrel took a deep breath, ready to use her skills to track it down. The adventure had just begun.
