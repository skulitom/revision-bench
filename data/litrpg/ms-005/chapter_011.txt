The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across Highcut's cobblestone streets. Sorrel adjusted the straps of her worn leather satchel, feeling the familiar weight of the salt pouch inside. She'd been wandering these parts for a while now, trying to make sense of the strange happenings since she first crossed into this world.

As she rounded a corner, she noticed Bex leaning against an ornate fountain, her eyes scanning the sky with a practiced ease. Sorrel approached cautiously, her Quickstep skill ready in case she needed to make a swift exit.

"Hey, Bex," Sorrel called out, trying to keep her voice steady. "You know anything about this place?"

Bex turned, a small smile playing on her lips. "Depends on what you're looking for."

Sorrel nodded, grateful for the chance to talk to someone who seemed to know their way around. "I'm trying to figure out where I can use my skills best. And maybe find some gear."

Bex's eyes flickered with understanding. "You might want to check out the old clock tower. There's a guy there who trades in some interesting stuff."

Sorrel's interest piqued. "Like what?"

"A brass compass," Bex said, nodding towards the tower. "It's said to point you in the direction of your heart's desire. Or at least, it points you somewhere useful."

Sorrel felt a thrill of anticipation. A compass could be just what she needed to navigate this world more effectively. "Thanks, Bex. I'll check it out."

As Sorrel made her way towards the clock tower, she couldn't shake the feeling that this was exactly where she needed to be. With her skills and a new brass compass in hand, she was ready to face whatever challenges lay ahead.
