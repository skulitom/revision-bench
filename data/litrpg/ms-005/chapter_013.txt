The Sallow Wood was a place of muted greens and whispering shadows. Sorrel moved cautiously through the underbrush, her senses heightened by the quiet rustle of leaves. She could feel the presence of Maro, an enigmatic figure whose motives remained as elusive as the shifting patterns of light filtering through the canopy.

With a practiced flick of her wrist, Sorrel activated Echo Sight. The world around her shimmered with a spectral overlay, revealing hidden pathways and potential threats. It was in this enhanced state that she noticed Maro's subtle movements—a dance between visibility and shadow.

"Maro," Sorrel called out, her voice steady despite the tension. "We need to talk."

The figure paused, turning to face her. His eyes held a depth that seemed almost otherworldly, yet there was an undeniable familiarity in his stance. "Sorrel," he replied, his tone carrying a hint of amusement. "You're always so direct."

Before she could respond, Sorrel's instincts kicked in. She sensed a disturbance in the air—a shift that hinted at danger. Without hesitation, she activated Quickstep, her movements fluid and precise as she maneuvered around the fallen logs and thickets.

As they continued their walk, Maro spoke of ancient lore and hidden truths, his words weaving a tapestry of intrigue. Sorrel listened intently, her mind racing with possibilities. She knew that understanding his intentions was key to navigating the challenges ahead.

In a moment of clarity, Sorrel realized that she needed more than just agility and foresight. The world demanded resilience, a way to bind and protect. It was then that she felt a surge of power coursing through her veins—a new skill awakening within her.

With a deep breath, Sorrel focused her energy, channeling it into the very earth beneath her feet. Vines sprang forth, intertwining with the roots and branches around them. Thorn Coil had been learned.

Maro watched in silent acknowledgment, his expression unreadable. "You're growing stronger," he observed, his voice carrying a note of respect.

Sorrel nodded, feeling the weight of her new abilities. She was ready to face whatever lay ahead, her resolve as steadfast as the wood around her.

As they continued their journey through the Sallow Wood, Sorrel knew that this was just the beginning. With each step, she was becoming more than she ever imagined—a force to be reckoned with in a world of magic and mystery.
