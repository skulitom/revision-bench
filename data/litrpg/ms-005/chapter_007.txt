The Ninth Terrace was a place of constant motion, with its stone pathways weaving through towering cliffs and cascading waterfalls. Sorrel moved with practiced ease, her feet barely touching the ground as she used Quickstep to navigate the terrain. The air here was charged with an energy that seemed to hum in harmony with her heartbeat.

Her companion, Bex, was a few steps ahead, examining a peculiar rock formation. "Sorrel, look at this," Bex called out, holding up a shimmering stone that seemed to pulse with light. "It's like nothing I've seen before."

Sorrel approached, her curiosity piqued. "What do you think it is?" she asked, her eyes scanning the surroundings.

Bex shrugged. "No idea, but it feels important."

As they stood there, Sorrel felt a familiar tingle in her mind—a sign that something was about to change. She closed her eyes, focusing on the sensation. The world around her seemed to fade, replaced by echoes of past and future events. It was as if she could see through time itself.

When she opened her eyes, everything was clearer. She could sense the presence of hidden paths and secret treasures, like whispers carried on the wind. "This is amazing," Sorrel breathed, realizing she had just unlocked a new skill—Echo Sight.

Her excitement was cut short by a sudden rustling in the bushes nearby. Sorrel tensed, ready to react. "Bex, stay alert," she warned, her mind already racing with possibilities.

As they prepared for whatever might come next, Sorrel couldn't help but feel a surge of confidence. She had reached level 3, and with Echo Sight now at her disposal, the Ninth Terrace was no longer just a place to explore—it was a realm of endless potential.
