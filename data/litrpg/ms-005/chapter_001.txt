The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across the bustling streets of Highcut. Sorrel adjusted her backpack, feeling the familiar weight of the cracked whetstone nestled among her supplies. She had been wandering these streets for hours, trying to make sense of her new reality.

As she turned a corner, she came face-to-face with the Tallyman—a tall figure draped in a cloak that seemed to drink in the fading light. His eyes were sharp, like a hawk's, and they fixed on Sorrel with an intensity that made her heart skip a beat.

"Ah, a new level one," he said, his voice smooth as silk but carrying an edge of steel. "Welcome to Highcut. You have a lot to learn."

Sorrel swallowed hard, her mind racing. She knew she had to be careful around him; the Tallyman was known for his strict adherence to the rules. "I'm Sorrel," she said, trying to sound confident. "I'm just trying to figure things out."

The Tallyman nodded, a small smile playing on his lips. "Figuring things out is good. But remember, in Highcut, knowledge is power. And sometimes, the quickest way to learn is by doing."

Sorrel's mind flashed to her Quickstep skill—her only advantage right now. She could move swiftly, dodging obstacles and perhaps even the Tallyman's scrutiny. "I'll keep that in mind," she replied, hoping her tone didn't betray her nerves.

The Tallyman raised an eyebrow, as if reading her thoughts. "Good. Now, go explore. But be careful. Highcut is full of surprises."

With a nod, Sorrel turned and began to walk away, her steps light and quick. She knew she had to make the most of her Quickstep skill, not just to survive but to thrive in this strange new world. As she disappeared into the growing shadows, she couldn't help but wonder what other skills and items she might discover on her journey.
