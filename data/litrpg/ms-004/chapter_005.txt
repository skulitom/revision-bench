The air at the Cistern Gate was thick with mist and anticipation. Odile adjusted her grey cloak, feeling the weight of the bone charm against her chest—a constant reminder of the journey ahead. Bex stood beside her, eyes scanning the horizon for any sign of danger.

"Echo Sight," Odile murmured to herself, focusing on the skill that allowed her to perceive hidden threats. The world around her seemed to shimmer, revealing faint outlines of creatures lurking in the shadows. Her heart raced as she spotted a group of goblins skulking near the entrance.

Bex nodded, understanding the unspoken plan. "Let's take them out quietly," he suggested, his voice steady despite the tension.

Odile nodded, her mind already calculating the best approach. She reached into her inventory and pulled out the cracked whetstone, examining it closely. It was a curious find, its surface marred with tiny fractures that seemed to pulse with latent energy. Perhaps it could be useful later.

With a swift motion, she drew her sword, its blade glinting in the dim light. "Frost Nail," she whispered, conjuring an icy spike from her fingertips. The goblins were caught off guard as the frosty projectile pierced through the air, embedding itself in the ground near their feet.

The impact sent a shiver through the mist, momentarily freezing the goblins in place. Odile seized the opportunity, moving swiftly and silently towards them. Her agility was her greatest asset, allowing her to navigate the terrain with ease.

As she closed in, Bex stepped forward, his own weapon ready. Together, they formed an unbreakable front, their skills complementing each other perfectly. The goblins, realizing they were outmatched, began to retreat into the shadows from whence they came.

With a final glance at the cracked whetstone, Odile tucked it back into her inventory. It was a mystery she would have to unravel later, but for now, the immediate threat had been neutralized. As they moved deeper into the Cistern Gate, the mist began to clear, revealing new paths and possibilities.
