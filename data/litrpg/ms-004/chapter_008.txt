The air in the Drowned Steps was thick with moisture and mystery. Odile could feel the dampness seeping into her clothes as she cautiously stepped forward, her eyes scanning the surroundings. The eerie glow of bioluminescent algae cast a surreal light over the submerged ruins. Beside her, Nel moved with practiced ease, her voice barely above a whisper.

"Echo Sight," Odile murmured to herself, activating her skill. The world around her seemed to ripple, revealing hidden details and pathways that were otherwise invisible. She spotted a faint outline of a tunnel leading deeper into the labyrinthine structure. "This way," she signaled to Nel, who nodded in agreement.

As they navigated through the narrow passageway, Odile's mind was on high alert. The Drowned Steps were known for their treacherous traps and lurking dangers. Suddenly, a low growl echoed through the corridor, sending chills down her spine. Without hesitation, she drew her Frost Nail skill, ready to defend.

The source of the sound revealed itself—a massive, shadowy creature with razor-sharp teeth and glowing eyes. Odile's heart raced as she prepared for battle. She launched a Frost Nail, the icy projectile arcing through the air and striking the beast on its flank. The creature recoiled, its growl turning into a pained yelp.

Nel moved in close, her blade flashing with precision. Together, they fought back-to-back, their movements synchronized despite the chaos around them. Odile's mind was clear, focused solely on survival and victory.

As the battle raged on, Odile's hand instinctively reached for the bone charm in her pocket. It felt warm against her skin, a comforting reminder of her past and the strength she carried within. With one final, decisive strike using Thorn Coil, she incapacitated the creature, allowing them to press forward.

The cracked whetstone they had been searching for was nowhere to be seen, lost in the chaos of their encounter. But for now, survival was enough. They continued deeper into the Drowned Steps, ready for whatever lay ahead.
