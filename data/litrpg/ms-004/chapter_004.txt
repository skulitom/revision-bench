The damp air of Underbridge clung to Odile's skin as she stepped cautiously over the slick stones. The flickering torches cast eerie shadows that danced along the walls, their light barely penetrating the thick mist. Her heart pounded a steady rhythm against her ribs, a reminder of the adventure that awaited her.

A figure emerged from the shadows, his presence commanding and imposing. Warden Ott stood before her, his eyes sharp and discerning. "Odile," he began, his voice echoing slightly in the confined space. "You've shown promise. Today, you will learn a new skill."

Odile nodded, her mind racing with anticipation. She had longed for this moment since she first discovered her Echo Sight, allowing her to perceive the echoes of past events. But Frost Nail was something else entirely—a skill that promised power and precision.

"Focus," Ott instructed, gesturing towards a nearby target. "Visualize the chill, the sharpness. Let it flow from your core."

Odile closed her eyes, taking a deep breath. She imagined a frigid wind coursing through her veins, crystallizing into a solid form. With a flick of her wrist, she unleashed a shard of ice, watching as it struck true and shattered the target with a satisfying crack.

The warden nodded in approval. "Well done, Odile. You have earned your level."

As the words settled in, Odile felt a surge of energy ripple through her. She was no longer just a novice; she was a warrior with newfound abilities. With a grateful smile, she turned to leave, her grey cloak fluttering behind her like a shadow.

The Underbridge had tested her, but she emerged stronger, ready for whatever lay ahead.
