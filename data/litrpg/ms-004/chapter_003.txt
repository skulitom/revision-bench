The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across Highcut's bustling streets. Odile adjusted her grey cloak, feeling its familiar weight against her shoulders. She had been wandering these parts for a while, trying to understand how she ended up here—this strange place where skills like hers were more than just figments of imagination.

As she made her way through the throng of people, her mind focused on Echo Sight, the skill that allowed her to see echoes of recent events. It was a gift, or perhaps a curse, but it had proven invaluable in navigating this world. She could almost hear the faint whispers of what had happened just moments before, like echoes bouncing off invisible walls.

Suddenly, she noticed Bex, a local who seemed to know more about Highcut than anyone else. Bex's eyes caught hers, and with a nod, he beckoned her over. "Odile," he greeted, his voice carrying an air of mystery. "I've been expecting you."

Curiosity piqued, Odile approached. "What do you mean?" she asked, trying to keep her tone casual.

Bex smiled knowingly. "You have the skill to see what others can't. It's time you learned more." He reached into his pocket and pulled out a small, intricately carved bone charm. "This will help you hone your Echo Sight."

Odile took the charm, feeling its weight in her palm. It was oddly comforting, like it belonged with her all along. "Thank you," she said, her voice steady despite the rush of excitement.

Bex nodded again. "Just remember, with great power comes great responsibility. Use it wisely."

As she tucked the new bone charm into her pocket alongside the old one, Odile felt a surge of determination. Highcut was full of mysteries, and she was ready to uncover them all.
