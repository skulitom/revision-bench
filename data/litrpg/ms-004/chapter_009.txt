The Cistern Gate loomed before Odile, its ancient stones whispering secrets of the past. She adjusted her grip on the bone charm, feeling its familiar weight in her palm. The air was thick with anticipation as she stepped forward, Echo Sight already flickering to life in her mind's eye.

"Odile," a deep voice rumbled, and she turned to see Warden Ott standing beside the gate. His presence was imposing, yet there was an underlying curiosity in his gaze. "You're here for the trial, I presume?"

Nodding, Odile replied, "Yes, Warden. I'm ready." Her mind scanned the area, Echo Sight revealing hidden pathways and potential threats lurking in the shadows.

Warden Ott raised an eyebrow, a hint of amusement in his tone. "Be careful. The Cistern Gate is more than it seems. Many have entered, but few have returned."

Odile's heart raced, but she kept her expression calm. She knew the risks, yet the thrill of the unknown was irresistible. "I understand," she said, stepping closer to the gate. "What should I expect?"

Warden Ott gestured towards the massive stone doors. "Inside, you'll find trials that test your skills. Use them wisely."

As the gates began to creak open, Odile felt a surge of determination. She would face whatever lay beyond with her Echo Sight guiding her, Frost Nail at the ready for any icy encounters, and Thorn Coil prepared to ensnare her foes.

With a deep breath, she stepped through the Cistern Gate, the bone charm pulsing softly against her skin. The adventure was just beginning.
