The Cistern Gate loomed before Odile, its ancient stones whispering secrets of the ages. Bex stood beside her, eyes darting nervously over the moss-covered archway. "This is it," Bex muttered, "the entrance to the Underrealm."

Odile nodded, feeling a familiar thrill at the prospect of what lay ahead. She reached into her pocket and fingered the bone charm, its worn surface a reminder of her journey thus far. "Let's do this," she said, her voice steady.

The air around them was thick with anticipation as Odile activated Echo Sight. The world seemed to ripple, revealing hidden paths and lurking dangers. A faint outline of a shadowy figure appeared near the gate, its intentions unclear. Odile tightened her grip on the charm, ready for whatever might come.

Bex hesitated, then took a deep breath. "I'm with you," she said, determination in her voice.

As they approached the gate, the shadowy figure moved, stepping into the light. It was a creature of the Underrealm, eyes glowing with malice. Odile's heart raced, but she knew she had to act quickly.

Without thinking, she unleashed Frost Nail, sending a shard of ice spiraling towards the creature. The impact was powerful, freezing it in place. Bex gasped, impressed by Odile's skill.

But the creature was resilient, its form shifting as it tried to break free. Odile needed more than just frost. She focused, drawing on her training and experience. A surge of energy coursed through her veins, and she felt a new power awakening within her.

With a deep breath, she channeled her newfound strength into Iron Skin. The sensation was exhilarating, as if an invisible armor enveloped her, making her feel invincible. She could sense the creature's attempts to harm her, but they were futile against her reinforced defenses.

The creature struggled, but Odile remained calm, knowing she had gained a crucial advantage. As it finally succumbed to the cold and her unyielding defense, Bex clapped her on the back, admiration evident in her eyes.

Odile smiled, feeling a sense of accomplishment. She had reached level 4, and with Iron Skin now part of her arsenal, she was ready for whatever challenges lay ahead in the Underrealm.
