Chapter 6

The Cistern Gate loomed before Odile, its ancient stones whispering secrets of the past. Maro stood beside her, his eyes scanning the horizon with a practiced wariness. The air was thick with anticipation, and Odile felt the familiar tingle of adventure coursing through her veins.

"Maro," she began, her voice steady despite the flutter in her stomach, "I think I should use Echo Sight here. There might be hidden threats lurking around."

Maro nodded, his expression serious. "Good call. Let's see what it reveals."

Odile closed her eyes, focusing her mind. The world faded to a soft hum as she activated her skill. Images of the area played out before her like a silent movie. She saw a narrow alley to their left, a potential shortcut, but also noticed a shadowy figure darting between the buildings—a possible threat.

"Maro, there's someone moving around back there," Odile said, pointing to the alley. "We should be cautious."

Maro gave a curt nod and moved to her side, his hand resting on the hilt of his sword. They decided to take the longer route around the gate, avoiding any unnecessary risks.

As they walked, Odile's fingers brushed against the bone charm in her pocket, a comforting weight. She had always felt a strange connection to it, though she didn't know why. Her gaze fell on the cracked whetstone in her other hand. It was old and worn, but she couldn't part with it.

Suddenly, a chill ran down her spine. The air shifted, and Odile's heart skipped a beat. She instinctively reached for her Frost Nail skill, ready to defend herself if needed. But before she could act, the ground beneath them trembled slightly, and a low rumble echoed through the gate.

Maro's eyes widened. "Something's coming."

Odile nodded, her mind racing. She knew they had to be prepared. As they moved forward, she couldn't shake the feeling that something significant was about to happen—something that would change everything.

And then, without warning, her grey cloak slipped from her shoulders, caught by a sudden gust of wind. It fluttered away into the distance, disappearing behind the gate. Odile watched it go, a small pang of loss in her chest. But there was no time to dwell on it now. The adventure was just beginning.
