Odile stood at the edge of Kellsmarket, her senses tingling with anticipation. The bustling marketplace was alive with the hum of activity, merchants hawking their wares and adventurers sharing tales of distant lands. She adjusted her grey cloak, feeling its familiar weight against her shoulders.

She had only just arrived in this vibrant city, a place teeming with opportunity and danger alike. Her goal was simple: learn more about Echo Sight, the skill she possessed but barely understood. It promised to reveal hidden truths, but how it worked remained a mystery.

As she navigated through the throng of people, her eyes caught sight of Warden Ott, a figure known for his wisdom and knowledge of arcane matters. She approached him cautiously, aware that gaining his attention would require both tact and confidence.

"Excuse me, Warden Ott," Odile called out, her voice steady despite the flutter in her chest. "I couldn't help but notice your reputation for understanding the unseen."

Ott turned, his eyes narrowing slightly as he assessed her. "And what brings you to seek my counsel?" he asked, his tone both curious and guarded.

Odile took a deep breath, steadying herself. "I possess a skill called Echo Sight," she began, her intellect guiding her words carefully. "But I'm unsure of its full potential."

Warden Ott's expression softened slightly, a hint of intrigue replacing the initial skepticism. "Echo Sight, you say? A rare ability indeed. Perhaps there is more to learn here than you realize."

As they spoke, Odile felt a subtle vibration in her mind, as if Echo Sight was responding to the presence of someone who understood its mysteries. She knew this encounter could be the key to unlocking its secrets, and she was determined to explore every possibility.

With a nod of acknowledgment, Warden Ott gestured for her to follow him deeper into the market, where they could speak without interruption. Odile felt a surge of excitement; this was just the beginning of her journey into the unknown.
