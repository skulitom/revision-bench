The Sallow Wood was a place of whispered secrets and dappled light, where shadows danced with the breeze. Odile moved cautiously through the underbrush, her senses heightened by the unfamiliar surroundings. The rustle of leaves seemed to carry a thousand stories, each one beckoning her closer. Her grey cloak flowed gently behind her, blending seamlessly with the muted tones of the forest.

Ahead, Warden Ott stood tall and imposing, his presence like a sentinel guarding the ancient trees. His eyes were sharp, scanning the horizon as if searching for something—or someone—lost to time. Odile approached him, her steps light on the soft earth. 

"Odile," he greeted, his voice deep and resonant. "You've come far. The Sallow Wood is a place of both wonder and danger."

Odile nodded, feeling the weight of his words settle over her like a mantle. She knew she had to tread carefully here, where every sound could be a friend or foe. Her mind turned to her Echo Sight, the skill that allowed her to perceive echoes of past events in the environment. It was a gift, one that she hoped would guide her through the mysteries of the wood.

"Echo Sight," she murmured under her breath, closing her eyes briefly as she focused her thoughts. The world around her seemed to shift subtly, whispers of memories playing at the edges of her perception. She could sense the echoes of footsteps long since faded, and the remnants of laughter carried on the wind.

Warden Ott watched her intently, a knowing glint in his eye. "Use it wisely," he advised. "The Sallow Wood holds many secrets, some best left undisturbed."

Odile opened her eyes, determination etched into her features. She was ready to uncover what lay hidden here, guided by the echoes of the past and the wisdom of those who had walked this path before her.
