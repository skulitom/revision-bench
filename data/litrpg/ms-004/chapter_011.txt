The dense canopy of the Sallow Wood filtered the sunlight into a mosaic of shadows and light. Odile moved quietly through the underbrush, her senses heightened by the forest's subtle whispers. The air was thick with the scent of damp earth and decaying leaves, a constant reminder of nature's cycle.

Ahead, Maro, a rugged figure with a stern expression, stood guard. His presence was reassuring, yet his eyes betrayed an unease that mirrored Odile's own. She approached him cautiously, her hand resting on the bone charm in her pocket, feeling its familiar weight.

"Maro," she began, her voice steady despite the tension. "I think we should scout ahead. There might be more to this place than meets the eye."

Maro nodded, his gaze scanning the surroundings. "Agreed. But let's stay alert. This forest has a way of hiding its secrets."

Odile activated Echo Sight, the world around her transforming into a symphony of echoes and vibrations. She could sense the subtle movements of creatures hidden among the trees, their intentions unclear. The skill was invaluable in navigating such an unpredictable environment.

As they moved deeper into the wood, a rustling sound caught Odile's attention. Her heart quickened as she focused her Echo Sight, tracing the source of the disturbance. It was a small creature, darting between the shadows.

"Stay back," she warned Maro, her hand instinctively reaching for the Frost Nail skill. The air around her chilled, and a delicate frost began to form at her fingertips. She released the skill, a shimmering ice spike arcing gracefully towards the creature.

The creature paused, its eyes wide with surprise before it darted away, unharmed but clearly deterred. Odile let out a breath she hadn't realized she'd been holding. "That'll teach you to be nosy," she muttered, her lips curling into a small smile.

Maro chuckled, clapping her on the back. "Good work, Odile. Let's keep moving."

With renewed determination, they pressed on, ready to face whatever the Sallow Wood had in store for them.
