The Ninth Terrace stretched out before Odile, a labyrinth of stone and shadow. The air was thick with the scent of moss and ancient secrets. Sister Elspeth stood beside her, her eyes scanning the horizon with an intensity that matched Odile's own.

"Remember," Elspeth said, her voice a soft murmur, "the path is rarely straight. Use your Echo Sight to find what's hidden."

Odile nodded, feeling the familiar hum of the skill as it activated. The world around her seemed to pulse with hidden energy, revealing paths and traps that were invisible to the naked eye. She followed the echoes, each one a whisper guiding her steps.

As they moved deeper into the terrace, the ground beneath them shifted subtly. Odile's Quickstep kicked in, allowing her to navigate the treacherous terrain with ease. Her feet barely touched the ground as she danced across the stones, her movements fluid and precise.

Suddenly, a low growl echoed through the air, and a shadowy figure emerged from the trees. Odile raised her hand, Frost Nail ready. The chill of the skill spread through her fingers, and a spear of ice shot forth, striking the creature with a crackling force that sent it stumbling back into the shadows.

Elspeth smiled, clapping Odile on the shoulder. "Well done. Now, let's find what we're looking for."

They continued their journey, each step revealing more of the terrace's secrets. Finally, they reached a clearing where an ancient altar stood, surrounded by inscriptions that seemed to glow with an otherworldly light.

Odile approached cautiously, her Iron Skin shielding her from any residual magic. She traced the symbols with her fingers, feeling a connection to something ancient and powerful.

Elspeth handed her a folded map, its edges worn and delicate. "This should help," she said, her eyes twinkling with excitement. "It's time to uncover what lies beneath."

Odile tucked the map into her inventory, feeling a surge of anticipation. The Ninth Terrace held many secrets, but with her skills and this new guide, she was ready to uncover them all.
