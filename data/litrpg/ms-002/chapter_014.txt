The Sallow Wood was a place of eerie beauty, its twisted branches casting long, gnarled shadows on the forest floor. Odile moved through it with practiced ease, her senses alert to every rustle and whisper of the wind. Beside her, Nel's presence was both comforting and unnerving; the air around him seemed charged with an untamed energy.

"Odile," Nel said, his voice barely above a murmur, "we need to be careful. The forest is thick with unseen eyes."

She nodded, her mind already calculating their next move. The bone charm had been their guide, but now it was lost, and the path forward was uncertain. She reached into her inventory, fingers brushing against the cool metal of the iron key. It might not unlock a door, but it could be useful in ways she hadn't yet considered.

"Let's try Ash Beckon," Odile suggested, her eyes scanning the surroundings for any sign of danger. The skill would allow them to call upon the forest itself, to bend its will to their purpose. She closed her eyes, focusing her intellect and vitality into a single point of concentration.

As she channeled her energy, the air around them seemed to shimmer, the very essence of the Sallow Wood responding to her call. Leaves rustled without wind, and the ground beneath their feet felt alive. Nel watched in awe as Odile's mind Spike connected with the forest's consciousness, seeking guidance and protection.

The forest responded with a gentle hum, a sound that resonated deep within her bones. It was a song of ancient wisdom, a Glass Song that whispered secrets only she could understand. She opened her eyes, feeling a renewed sense of purpose.

"We have a way forward," she said, turning to Nel with determination in her gaze. "Let's move."

With Quickstep, they advanced through the Sallow Wood, their path illuminated by the forest's unseen light. The bone charm was gone, but Odile knew that as long as she had her skills and her wits, they would find their way.
