The air was thick with the scent of damp earth and old stone as Odile stood beneath the Underbridge. The low arch of the structure cast long shadows, making it feel like a different world altogether. She glanced around, her eyes landing on Sister Elspeth, who was waiting patiently beside a small wooden table.

"Welcome, Odile," Sister Elspeth greeted with a warm smile. "I've been expecting you."

Odile nodded, trying to suppress the nervous flutter in her stomach. This was it—the first step of her journey into the unknown. She clutched the sealed vial in her pocket, its contents a mystery even to her.

"Are you ready?" Sister Elspeth asked, her voice gentle but firm.

Odile took a deep breath, feeling her heart pound against her ribs. "I think so," she replied, trying to sound more confident than she felt.

Sister Elspeth nodded approvingly. "Remember, Odile, your Silent Palm is a powerful tool. Use it wisely."

Odile closed her eyes for a moment, picturing the skill in her mind—her hand moving with precision and grace, striking without making a sound. She could feel the power coiling within her, waiting to be unleashed.

"Let's begin," Sister Elspeth said, standing up and gesturing towards the path that snaked into the darkness beyond the bridge.

Odile took one last look at the safety of the Underbridge before stepping forward. The world ahead was full of possibilities, and she was ready to embrace them all. With Silent Palm as her guide, she knew she could face whatever challenges lay in wait.
