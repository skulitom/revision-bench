Chapter 9

[ STATUS ]  
  Name: Odile  
  Level: 3  
  Strength: 15

  Skills: Silent Palm, Quickstep, Ash Beckon  
  Inventory: sealed vial  

The cool air of the Underbridge sent a shiver down Odile's spine as she scanned the dimly lit area. Nel was already there, her eyes darting around like a cat on the prowl. "Odile, over here," Nel called softly, her voice barely above a whisper. 

Odile approached cautiously, her mind on high alert. The Underbridge was notorious for its hidden dangers and unpredictable inhabitants. She tucked her sealed vial deeper into her pocket, feeling its cool surface against her thigh.

Nel gestured towards the shadows ahead. "I think we've got company," she said, her tone laced with caution. Odile nodded, her hand instinctively reaching for the vial in her inventory. 

"Let's move quietly," Odile suggested, activating her Quickstep skill. Her feet moved silently across the ground, each step calculated to avoid any noise. Nel followed suit, her movements equally graceful.

As they approached the shadows, Odile prepared to use her Silent Palm. She could feel the tension in the air, a silent warning that whatever lurked there was not friendly. 

Suddenly, a rustling sound broke the silence. Odile tensed, ready to strike if necessary. But before she could react, Nel stepped forward and used Ash Beckon, calling forth a cloud of ash from the ground. The sudden burst of smoke obscured their vision, buying them precious seconds.

In that moment, Odile knew they had to act fast. She moved closer, her heart pounding in her chest. With a swift motion, she unleashed her Silent Palm, striking with precision and force. The target was down before they could even react.

As the ash settled, Nel gave Odile a nod of approval. "Good work," she said, her voice carrying a hint of relief. Odile smiled, feeling a surge of confidence. Together, they were ready to face whatever else the Underbridge had in store.
