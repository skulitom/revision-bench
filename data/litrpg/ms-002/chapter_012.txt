Odile stood at the edge of Kellsmarket, her eyes scanning the bustling crowd. The market was alive with vendors shouting their wares and adventurers haggling over enchanted items. She adjusted the bone charm around her neck, feeling its familiar weight against her skin. Her mind flickered to the iron key in her pocket, a remnant of her latest quest.

Warden Ott approached, his stern gaze softened by a hint of curiosity. "Odile, I've been meaning to speak with you," he began, his voice carrying an air of authority. "There's been talk of unrest among the traders. Rumors of a hidden threat."

Odile nodded, her mind already racing through possibilities. "I can look into it," she offered, her voice steady.

"Good." Ott paused, then added, "There's something else. A merchant claims to have information about a rare artifact. It might be connected to your quest."

Odile's interest piqued. "Where is this merchant?"

"In the eastern stalls," Ott replied, pointing towards a cluster of tents. "But be careful. The market can be unpredictable."

With a silent nod, Odile activated her Quickstep skill, her movements fluid as she weaved through the crowd. Her agility served her well, allowing her to navigate the throng with ease. She reached the eastern stalls and found the merchant, an old man with eyes that seemed to hold centuries of secrets.

"Odile," he greeted, a knowing smile on his lips. "I've been expecting you."

She nodded, her mind focused. "What do you know about the artifact?"

The old man leaned in, lowering his voice. "It's said to be hidden within the ruins of the Old City. But there's more. A bone charm like yours is needed to unlock its secrets."

Odile's heart raced. This was exactly what she needed. "How can I acquire another charm?"

The merchant handed her a small, intricately carved bone charm. "This one is yours," he said, his voice tinged with mystery.

Odile accepted it, feeling the familiar energy pulse through her fingers. With a grateful nod, she activated Silent Palm, ensuring their conversation remained private amidst the bustling market noise.

"Thank you," she whispered before slipping away, her mind already plotting her next move. The ruins awaited, and with this new charm, she felt one step closer to uncovering the artifact's secrets.
