The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across the cobblestones of Highcut. Kaelen leaned against the cool stone wall of the inn, his mind replaying the events of the day. Sister Elspeth had been both a guide and a challenge, her presence in the city as formidable as ever.

"Kaelen," she called, approaching with a gentle yet authoritative stride. "You've done well today, but there's more to learn."

He nodded, feeling the familiar rush of adrenaline at the prospect of growth. "What's next?"

Sister Elspeth smiled, her eyes twinkling with knowledge. "Quickstep. It's time you mastered this skill."

Kaelen closed his eyes, focusing on the sensation of movement, the way his feet had danced across the battlefield earlier. He could almost feel the rhythm in his veins.

"Imagine your steps as a series of precise, calculated movements," she instructed. "Each one bringing you closer to your target, faster and more efficiently."

He visualized it, feeling the energy coalesce within him. With a deep breath, he opened his eyes and nodded.

Sister Elspeth clapped her hands together softly. "Very good. Now, let's put it into practice."

They moved through the bustling streets of Highcut, Kaelen's steps becoming fluid and graceful under her guidance. He felt a surge of power, a newfound agility that made him feel almost weightless.

As they reached a quiet alleyway, Sister Elspeth paused. "There. Now, use Quickstep to reach that ledge."

Kaelen focused, feeling the skill settle into his muscle memory. With a swift motion, he leaped, his feet landing with precision on the narrow ledge above. He looked down at Sister Elspeth, who nodded approvingly.

"You've done it," she said, her voice filled with pride. "Welcome to level 3, Kaelen."

A sense of accomplishment washed over him. He was ready for whatever Highcut had in store next.
