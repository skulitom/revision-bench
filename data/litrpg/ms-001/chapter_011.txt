The Drowned Steps were a treacherous place, even by the standards of this twisted realm. The air was thick with mist, and the sound of water trickling echoed through the narrow passageway. Kaelen's footsteps were cautious, each step sending ripples across the slick, moss-covered stones beneath his feet. His eyes darted around, searching for any sign of movement in the dim light.

Suddenly, a shadow flickered at the edge of his vision. The Tallyman, a spectral figure with a ledger that seemed to absorb the very essence of life itself, materialized before him. Kaelen's heart raced, but he knew he couldn't afford to hesitate.

"Who dares disturb my accounts?" the Tallyman's voice echoed, cold and detached.

Kaelen took a deep breath, steadying his nerves. "I'm just passing through," he replied, trying to sound casual despite the danger.

The Tallyman raised an eyebrow, his gaze piercing. "Many have tried to pass, few have succeeded."

Kaelen knew he needed to act fast. He drew on Quickstep, feeling his agility surge as he sidestepped, creating distance between himself and the ominous figure. The Tallyman's eyes narrowed, but Kaelen was already thinking ahead.

He reached into his inventory, feeling the familiar weight of the bone charm in his hand. With a flick of his wrist, he activated Stone Ward, the ground beneath him solidifying into a protective barrier. The Tallyman hesitated, his ledger momentarily forgotten as he assessed the new obstacle.

Kaelen saw his chance. He focused on the Tallyman's mind, attempting to pierce through the spectral defenses with Mind Spike. The effort was intense, but he managed to disrupt the Tallyman's concentration long enough to slip past him.

As he moved forward, Kaelen noticed a glint of metal in the corner of his eye. Approaching cautiously, he found a tin lantern abandoned on a stone ledge. With a sense of relief, he added it to his inventory, knowing it would be invaluable in navigating the darkness ahead.

The Drowned Steps stretched out before him, but Kaelen felt a newfound confidence. He had faced the Tallyman and emerged victorious, ready for whatever lay beyond.
