Kaelen's first steps into Kellsmarket were tentative, the cobblestones beneath his boots unfamiliar. The market buzzed with activity, a cacophony of haggling voices and clinking coins that filled the air like an intoxicating melody. Stalls lined the streets, vibrant tapestries flapping in the breeze, each one promising treasures untold. Among the throng, Kaelen spotted Nel, a fellow novice adventurer whose reputation preceded him.

"Hey, Kaelen!" Nel called out, his voice cutting through the noise like a sharp blade. "First time here? This place can be overwhelming."

Kaelen nodded, feeling a mix of excitement and anxiety. He clutched his cracked whetstone tighter, an odd sense of attachment to the mundane item. It was all he had, yet it felt like a talisman of sorts.

"Mind Spike," he muttered under his breath, recalling the skill he'd honed in his brief time at the academy. It was his only weapon against the challenges ahead, a mental assault that could incapacitate foes with a mere thought.

Nel grinned, his eyes twinkling with mischief. "You'll need more than that to survive here, but it's a start. Follow me, I know a place where you can upgrade your gear."

As they weaved through the market, Kaelen felt a surge of determination. This was just the beginning, and he was ready to face whatever lay ahead. With Nel by his side, he knew he could navigate the chaos of Kellsmarket and beyond.
