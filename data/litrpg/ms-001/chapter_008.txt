The Underbridge was a shadowy expanse, its arches whispering secrets of travelers long past. Kaelen's boots echoed softly against the damp stone as he navigated the narrow path, the faint glow from his lantern barely cutting through the mist. Sister Elspeth stood beside him, her presence both comforting and enigmatic in this dim realm.

"Kaelen," she began, her voice a gentle murmur that seemed to blend with the ambient sounds of the underbridge. "The path ahead is treacherous. You must remain vigilant."

He nodded, his mind already tracing the routes he might take, calculating risks and rewards. His hand instinctively reached for the cracked whetstone in his pouch, a reminder of battles past and the need to stay sharp—both literally and figuratively.

As they moved forward, Kaelen felt a subtle shift in the air, a change that prickled at his instincts. He paused, closing his eyes briefly, allowing his mind to focus. The skill of Mind Spike surged through him, a silent probe into the unseen dangers lurking nearby. Nothing stirred, but he remained alert.

Sister Elspeth continued her guidance, her words weaving a tapestry of caution and wisdom. "The compass you carry is more than just a tool," she said, gesturing to the brass compass in his inventory. "It holds the key to your next steps."

Kaelen's fingers brushed over the compass, feeling its intricate engravings. He opened it, watching as the needle spun before settling on a direction that seemed to pulse with intent. With a nod of understanding, he tucked the compass back into his pouch.

"Thank you, Sister," Kaelen replied, his voice steady. "I'll tread carefully."

As they ventured deeper into the Underbridge, the path ahead remained uncertain, but Kaelen felt a renewed sense of purpose. With his skills honed and his inventory secured, he was ready to face whatever lay beyond.
