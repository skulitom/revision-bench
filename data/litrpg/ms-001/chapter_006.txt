The Ninth Terrace stretched out before Kaelen, a vast expanse of lush greenery interspersed with winding paths and ancient stone structures. The air was thick with the scent of wildflowers and the distant hum of mystical energy. It was here, amidst this serene chaos, that Kaelen found himself, eyes scanning the surroundings for any sign of trouble.

Nel, a fellow adventurer with an air of confidence and a knack for finding trouble, approached him with a knowing grin. "You ready for what's ahead?" she asked, her voice carrying over the gentle rustle of leaves.

Kaelen nodded, feeling the familiar weight of his cracked whetstone in his pocket. "Let's see what this place has to offer," he replied, his mind already assessing potential threats and opportunities.

As they ventured deeper into the Terrace, Kaelen's instincts kicked in. He sensed a presence nearby—a creature lurking just out of sight, its intentions unclear. With a quick gesture, he activated his Mind Spike, focusing his thoughts to extend his awareness beyond the physical realm. The sensation was like a ripple spreading through his mind, reaching out into the unknown.

The creature revealed itself—a small, shadowy figure with eyes that glinted with mischief. It darted between trees, taunting them with its agility. Kaelen's pulse quickened, but he remained calm. This was his moment to shine.

"Blood Ledger," he whispered, drawing on his skill to track the creature's movements through the energy it left behind. The path it took became clear, etched in a trail of glowing lines only visible to him.

With Nel at his side, Kaelen followed the trail, each step bringing them closer to their quarry. As they closed in, the creature paused, eyeing them with a mix of curiosity and defiance. It was then that Kaelen spotted something—a small pouch lying on the ground, abandoned in the creature's haste.

He picked it up, examining the contents. Salt, he realized, its crystalline structure shimmering in the sunlight. A useful addition to his inventory, especially for warding off certain magical effects.

"Looks like we've got ourselves a little bonus," Nel remarked, her eyes twinkling with amusement.

Kaelen nodded, tucking the salt pouch into his pack. "Every bit helps," he said, already planning their next move. The Ninth Terrace was full of surprises, and they were just getting started.
