The damp air of the Cistern Gate clung to Kaelen's skin as he stood before Sister Elspeth. The ancient gate loomed over them, its stone arches whispering secrets of ages past. The flickering torchlight cast eerie shadows that danced across the cobblestones.

Sister Elspeth's eyes were sharp and discerning, her voice calm yet authoritative as she spoke. "Kaelen, you've come far for a novice. But this place demands more than just courage; it requires wit."

Kaelen nodded, feeling the weight of his cracked whetstone in his pocket—a reminder of the journey ahead. "I'm ready to prove myself," he replied, his voice steady despite the fluttering in his stomach.

The sister handed him a small, unassuming copper ring. "This is your key. It holds more power than you can imagine, but only if you learn to unlock its potential."

Kaelen took the ring, feeling its cool metal against his skin. He slipped it onto his finger, where it fit snugly. "What does it do?" he asked, curiosity piqued.

Sister Elspeth smiled cryptically. "That, my friend, is for you to discover. But remember, in this world, knowledge is as vital as strength."

Kaelen felt a surge of determination. He knew he had to rely on his wits and the one skill he possessed—Mind Spike. It was time to delve deeper into the mysteries of the Cistern Gate, guided by the enigmatic Sister Elspeth and the newly acquired copper ring.
