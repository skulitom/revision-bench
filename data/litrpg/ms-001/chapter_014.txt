The mist hung low over Fenmouth, shrouding the cobblestone streets in a ghostly embrace. Kaelen adjusted the straps of his grey cloak, feeling its weight settle comfortably around him. The chill of the early morning air nipped at his skin, but he was used to it by now. He glanced down at his inventory; the cracked whetstone and salt pouch were old friends, the brass compass a reliable guide through unknown lands.

Warden Ott stood before him, a stern figure in the dim light of dawn. His eyes, sharp as flint, surveyed Kaelen with an intensity that made the young adventurer's skin prickle. "You're here for the trial," Ott said, his voice carrying the weight of tradition and expectation.

Kaelen nodded, feeling the familiar rush of adrenaline. "I'm ready."

The warden handed him a new grey cloak, identical to the one he already wore. "This is yours now," Ott said, his tone leaving no room for argument. "It's been passed down through generations. It will protect you."

Kaelen accepted the cloak, feeling its fabric weave around him like a second skin. He could sense its history, the countless trials it had endured and survived. With a nod of gratitude, he tucked the original cloak into his pack, making room for the new one.

"Remember," Ott continued, "the trial is not just about strength or agility. It's about wit, endurance, and the will to overcome."

Kaelen nodded again, feeling a surge of determination. He knew the path ahead would be fraught with challenges, but he was ready. With his skills honed and his inventory stocked, he set off into the misty streets of Fenmouth, the grey cloak fluttering behind him like a banner of resolve.
