The sun was just beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across Highcut's cobblestone streets. Kaelen adjusted the strap of his tin lantern, its dim glow a comforting presence in the encroaching twilight. He had been wandering these familiar paths for hours, but today felt different—like the air was charged with anticipation.

As he rounded a corner, Kaelen spotted Warden Ott standing by the town fountain, his stern gaze scanning the area as if expecting trouble. Ott was a towering figure, his presence both reassuring and intimidating. Kaelen approached cautiously, knowing that the warden's mood could shift like the tides.

"Evening, Warden Ott," Kaelen greeted, nodding respectfully. "Anything I can help with?"

Ott turned, his eyes narrowing slightly as he studied Kaelen. "There have been whispers of trouble brewing in the outskirts," he said, his voice low and gravelly. "I need someone reliable to keep an eye on things. You're up for it?"

Kaelen nodded, feeling a familiar thrill at the prospect of adventure. "Count me in. What's the plan?"

Ott handed him the brass compass, its needle spinning slightly before settling northward. "This will guide you. There are reports of strange activity near the old mill. Use your skills wisely."

With the warden's instructions clear, Kaelen tucked the compass into his belt and checked his inventory. The cracked whetstone felt reassuringly solid in his hand, a reminder of battles past. He took a pinch of salt from the pouch, letting it linger on his tongue for a moment before swallowing—a small ritual for luck.

As he set off towards the outskirts, Kaelen felt the familiar rush of adrenaline. The night was young, and with Quickstep at his command, he knew he could cover ground swiftly. Whatever lay ahead, he was ready to face it head-on.
